| A — degree and subexponential security | The formal pp. 16–17 construction supports public degree d, unlike the overview on p. 14. Scaling one private factor through the stored flag-times-seed copy preserves private degree two. The explicit subexponential exponent is fixed from the assumption exponents and Params before the adversary. |
