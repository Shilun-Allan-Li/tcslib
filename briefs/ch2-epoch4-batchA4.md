## The stored-format contract and its limits (binding, from the A3 REPORT)

Consume these facts; do not re-establish, weaken, or contradict them.

- Each row has `l = 2*(k+1)` binary fields: `k+1` positive-move counts
  then `k+1` negative-move counts; coordinate zero is the virtual input
  head (stored shifted: the public input position is the stored value
  **plus one**), the rest are source work heads. Canonical little-endian
  `Nat.bits`, the inherited self-delimiting `pairEncode` field format,
  rows concatenated in increasing time order, **row time implicit in
  sequence position**.
- A position is positive-count minus negative-count. Two count pairs can
  encode the same position: **comparison is by cross-sums**
  (`clSigned_eq`; the native `clCmp*` computes the sums column by column)
  — never by raw-encoding equality.
- Outward input moves are clamped **before** choosing a counter; counts
  freeze after internal halt (`clCounts_halted`); the effects-first
  terminal transition is still executed by the inherited `clRefAction`
  before any administrative step.
- The reader's overwrite contract requires the old target word no longer
  than the new field; `clCounts_width_mono` discharges it while visiting
  a movement-count field in **increasing** source-time order.
  **Restarting a scan at the initial row requires an explicit target
  reset/cleanup or a new loader contract** — nothing of the sort is
  silently available. The format theorems are pure; there is no free
  indexed access — every stream-bit read is charged.
- `clRec_prepared` and the inherited seams are **prepared** `Cfg.ofWords`
  statements; none is a genuine native `initCfg` startup.
- The recording bounds are **not** the emitter's common budget `P`; that
  future bound covers startup, fuel, and every whole round, and is not
  the horizon `T`.
