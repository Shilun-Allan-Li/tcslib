coordinate enumeration is chosen. LPN.Raw.drawEquiv explicitly composes the symmetric

Disclosed term-list refinement:
PolyTerms sigma R is List (R × List sigma). Repeated variables represent exponents.
Its denotation sums C(coefficient) times the product of variable occurrences. This
avoids choosing a Finsupp support order and is adequate for the Phase B qGen target.
encTerms reuses BoolCircuit.encodeList (the recommended sweep row), with pairEncode
blocks around each variable and each complete term so variable-length words cannot
merge. PolyTerms.encInt is a sign bit followed by Nat.bits of natAbs; encFin is
Nat.bits of the finite coordinate. TermsEfficient is only a named Prop, with its
_iff reader and exact uniform input layout; no efficiency proof is attempted.
