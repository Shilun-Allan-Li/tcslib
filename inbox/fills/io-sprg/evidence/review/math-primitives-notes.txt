with rfl readers. Internal weighted add/mul/finite-sum/finite-product/C/X facts

Buckets.lean choices
The increasing rank counts smaller Fin indices in the same bucket. The grid map
is Option(Fin rc x Fin rc), using finProdFinEquiv.symm for ranks below rc*rc,
and none globally when overfull. The defining test spells out the finite exists
so its Decidable instance is constructive without global/local instances.
The overfull predicate still has its own iff reader.
Reason for Option: rc(0)=0 while m(0) may be nonzero. The proposed paper-style
total default point would inhabit an empty grid. This pre-M0 refinement preserves
rc, cap and all successful positions. Matrix entries are sums over outputs whose
label and optional position match; no collision-dependent selection is needed.
The support bound works for every placement: every nonzero matrix entry must
receive at least one nonzero correction from the supplied bad set.
Generic AddCommMonoid coefficients suffice; the support count adds DecidableEq.

SparseFactor.lean choices
sparseEntries uses List.finRange rows, flatMap the List.finRange columns, then
filters nonzero entries with decide. Each slot's left column has the stored entry
at its row; the right row has one at its column. Slots beyond list length are zero.
Semiring plus DecidableEq suffices. The factor function is total even when sparsity
fails, but reconstruction assumes at most t entries. A finite-cardinality variant
translates through sparseEntries_length_eq_card.

instances, efficiency proof, or foreign TCSlib unfolding occurs in these files.
