SP-W3: pre-M0 polynomial-stretch non-vacuity repair

Inherited draft obligation: Raw.HasPolyStretch S was only
  exists tau > 1, exists kappa0, forall kappa >= kappa0,
    (S.seedBits kappa : Real)^tau <= S.outLen kappa.
Binding r1 section 4.3 supplied that body; r2 retains it and r3 moves it to Raw.
No ruling expressly licenses bounded-length witnesses.

Counterexample: choose modulus (pi kappa).p and its growing residue width, but
pubLen=privLen=outLen=0, Unit indices and draws, the unique empty seed and output,
and explicit Unit-to-Fin-1 equivalences/encodings. All Raw width upper bounds and
nonempty fields can be met. seedBits=0 and tau=2 make the draft stretch inequality
0<=0. Real and random output laws coincide. HasComplexity is vacuous because its
output index is Fin 0. The raw existential conclusions would admit that witness
without using their cryptographic assumptions. Positivity alone is insufficient:
one-bit seed/output lengths with constant prime2 still satisfy 1^tau<=1.

Minimal robust repair selected before M0:
  exists tau > 1, exists kappa0, forall kappa >= kappa0,
    kappa <= S.seedBits kappa /\
      (S.seedBits kappa : Real)^tau <= S.outLen kappa.
The definition and its iff reader change together. The primitive wrapper and four
existential conclusions inherit the stronger meaning. No existential header,
algorithm, efficiency target, security assumption, or prime schedule changes.
Actual construction: pubLen=kappa*n+1>=1 and residueBits=kappa+1 imply the stronger
all-parameter bound kappa+1<=seedBits; add its weak form to the M2 proof.
The existing asymptotic exponent/threshold calculation is unchanged.

Authority: the user authorized best judgment while asleep, subject to modular,
adaptable code and downstream disclosure. E-ROUTE requires the exact obligation,
counterexample and minimal disclosed change; no E-SCOPE trigger applies. Both
original research agents independently confirmed the gap and repair. This is a
pre-freeze strengthening of an inherited design gap, not a silent weakening.
