## Reuse and orientation

The first simp step uses only the existing pointwise Layered.translation theorem.
It changes the real integrand to the simulated integrand at t - shift C x z.
ProbComp.expect_sample_equiv (Games/Probability.lean:304) already states exactly
that a finite uniform draw reindexed by an equivalence has the same expectation.
The chosen equivalence is Equiv.addRight (-shift C x z), a permutation of the
entire draw-function type. Its orientation sends t to t + (-shift C x z), so the
published group law sub_eq_add_neg identifies its argument with the required
subtraction. No inverse orientation or sign change is needed.

Equiv.addRight is the published additive version of Equiv.mulRight
(Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Units/Equiv.lean:116), and Equiv.coe_addRight is generated by
to_additive from coe_mulRight at line 120. Existing TCSlib consumers include
BonehBoyenSecurity/Coupling.lean:310, Homomorphic/GSW/Symmetric.lean:138 and
SecretSharing/Shamir.lean:158. Thus the draft uses its published application
reader rather than unfolding the equivalence. Equiv.subRight is the equally
applicable direct-subtraction alternative (generated from divRight at line 152),
but no second proof or duplicate helper is needed.

The proof does not unfold ProbComp.sample, ProbComp.expect, pgbCore, pgbStarCore,
shift or any other TCSlib definition. It does not construct a sampler, reprove a
uniform-bijection law or appeal to an approximate coupling. The finite additive
function space has the existing Fintype and Nonempty instances, including empty
index types and p = 1. No rank, nonzero, primeness or positive-dimension condition
is added. No mathematical obstruction was found.
