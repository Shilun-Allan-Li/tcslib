## Mixed-product specialization and singleton reindexing

Let X be the Fin l by Fin 1 matrix whose unique column is x. Two applications of the existing
`Matrix.mul_kronecker_mul` give, after `Matrix.one_mul`/`Matrix.mul_one`:

1. X ⊗ R = ((1 : Matrix (Fin l) (Fin l) _) ⊗ R) * (X ⊗ 1_J).
2. X ⊗ R = (X ⊗ 1_I) * ((1 : Matrix (Fin 1) (Fin 1) _) ⊗ R).

Their transitive equality is evaluated at row (i,u) and column (0,j). The left product's
intermediate index stays Fin l × J; its selected second factor is exactly publicTensor_J x
by `publicTensor_apply` and `Matrix.kroneckerMap_apply`.

The right product has intermediate index Fin 1 × I. `Fintype.sum_prod_type` exposes its
outer Fin 1 sum, and `Fin.sum_univ_one` selects coordinate 0. `Matrix.one_apply` then gives
the singleton identity entry 1 and `one_mul` removes it. The remaining sum is exactly
(publicTensor_I x * R) (i,u) j. Thus only the singleton-coordinate removal is performed
entrywise; no generic coordinate mixed-product proof or sum-product reproof is duplicated.

The final `simpa only` reads matrix multiplication and Kronecker entries through published
Mathlib equations and publicTensor through its public reader. The local X alias reduces
by definitional equality; neither core/Mathlib nor foreign TCSlib definitions are explicitly
passed to simp/rw/unfold.
