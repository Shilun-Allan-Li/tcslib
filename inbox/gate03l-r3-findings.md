The envelope bounds can be checked directly. Fix one work head, and write `p(v)` for its integer position at time `v`. The canonical seam and unit moves give

\[
p(0)=0,\qquad |p(s+1)-p(s)|\le 1,
\]
\[
|p(v)|
=\left|\sum_{s=0}^{v-1}(p(s+1)-p(s))\right|
\le\sum_{s=0}^{v-1}|p(s+1)-p(s)|
\le v.
\]

For `host_call_envelope`, `v ≤ t` immediately gives the advertised bound. For `Clean.envelope`, choose the clean first halt `t ≤ B(arg.length)`. If `v ≤ t`, the same inequality gives the budget bound. If `v > t`, absorbing halt and the terminal seam give `p(v)=p(t)=0`. For `host_clean_envelope`, apply the prefix inequality to the very duration supplied by `host_clean`, obtaining `abs(p(v)) ≤ v ≤ t ≤ B(arg.length)` throughout its inclusive prefix.

Finding 1 follows from the additional information `p(t)=0`. For `v ≤ t`,

\[
|p(v)|
=|p(v)-p(t)|
\le\sum_{s=v}^{t-1}|p(s+1)-p(s)|
\le t-v.
\]
\[
2|p(v)|\le v+(t-v)=t\le B(\mathrm{arg.length}),
\qquad
|p(v)|\le\left\lfloor\frac{B(\mathrm{arg.length})}{2}\right\rfloor.
\]

This is a mathematical derivation from the stated machine semantics, not a new Lean-checked lemma. It explains the documentation minor without requiring a stronger public API.

Adversarial instantiations:

- **Round-2 excursion, signed positions, and padding.** Use a two-tape module with seven states. Tape 0 and the native input head remain stationary; nothing is written or emitted. In states 0–2, tape 1 moves right; in states 3–5 it moves left; state 6 halts without moving. Its tape-1 positions at times 0 through 7 are `0,1,2,3,2,1,0,0`. For every input and argument it satisfies `Clean M id (fun _ => []) (fun _ => 7)`, including the empty argument. Reversing the moves gives the negative-position version. Both satisfy the new absolute-value bounds before, at and after halt. Embed it in a host with `K=3`, arbitrary output prefix and a return to a control state: the call ends at time 7, and the padded third head stays zero through that endpoint. A controller that subsequently moves this head right reaches position 8 at time 15, beyond the call budget. This does not contradict `host_clean_envelope`, whose bound ends at time 7; `host_call_envelope` at horizon 15 still gives the correct bound 15.
- **Zero horizon and degenerate budgets.** At `t=0`, `host_call_envelope` quantifies only over `v=0`, and every head is zero. With `K=0`, its head quantifier is empty, which is appropriate for a zero-work-tape host. This does not trivialize `host_clean_envelope`: cleanliness and padding imply `K≥1`. A budget with `B(0)=0` cannot satisfy cleanliness, since the empty argument would require `0<t≤0`.
- **Constant-time identity with arbitrarily long resident words.** A one-tape stationary module that halts on its first step without writing or emitting satisfies cleanliness with identity result and constant budget 1, for every argument length and native input. Every head stays zero. Thus the interface is inhabited and correctly bounds head positions without falsely bounding the length of the preloaded argument or output prefix by the call budget.
