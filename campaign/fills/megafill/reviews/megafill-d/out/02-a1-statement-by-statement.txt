## Lint check 8's blind spot (scripts/campaign_style_lint.py:207–252, read)

component starting with `<def>_` or `<lowerFirst def>_` (or `foo_` for `fooTM`). At the tip the
lint lists none of the five (probe/lint-complexity-tip.txt):

  the check the same way).

(2026-10-16): a definition counts as covered when any lemma is named after it"). The claim is
right, with transduce as a fifth instance.
