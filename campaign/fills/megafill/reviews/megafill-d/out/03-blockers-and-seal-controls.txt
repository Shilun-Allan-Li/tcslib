## Independent search for published eliminators / characterizations

scripts/statements.py lists every public theorem/lemma whose name or statement (up to `:=`,
comment-stripped) mentions TimeConstructible, SpaceConstructible, ComputesInSpace, logSpace or
transduce, at the tip and at the three open PR heads (probe/statements-mentioning.txt; the PR
heads add no such statement: the per-branch diff against the tip's list is empty). A grep for
`_def/_eq/_iff/_elim/_intro/_mk/_unfold/_nil/_cons` lemmas, `.def/.eq/.iff/...` dot lemmas and
`@[simp]`/`attribute [simp]` on the five names: 0 hits on all four revisions.

What exists, by role:
- TimeConstructible: introductions only (`timeConstructible_id`, `timeConstructible_poly`,
  `timeConstructible_two_pow`) and consumers taking it as a hypothesis (time_hierarchy,
  diagLang(Of)_mem_DTIME, oblivious_of_mem_DTIME, ntime_hierarchy, the two E targets). No
  statement yields the witness or the dominance. (Older files do eliminate it by anonymous
  constructor, e.g. Build/Primitives.lean:2351 and ClassNP/TMSAT.lean:1531, pre-rule-10 debt;
  a fill may not copy that.)
- SpaceConstructible: two instances (logSpace, n + 1) and consumers (savitch, ExpTime,
  ImmermanSzelepcsenyi, WordSize, Hierarchy). No closure or characterization lemma.
- ComputesInSpace: eliminations (`ComputesInSpace.computesFunInTime`,
  `.computesFunInTime_and_forall_spaceUsed_le`, `.k_eq_zero_of_exists_zero`), weakening
  (`ComputesInSpace.mono`), and introductions only for zero work tapes
  (`computesInSpace_zero_of_k_eq_zero`), from implicit logspace computability
  (`ImplicitlyLogspaceComputable.computesInSpace`, space c·logSpace, itself needing LOGSPACE
  membership) and the paused RAM layer (`computesInSpace_of_computes`, sorried). None introduces
  space c·(n^c + 1) for a machine with work tapes.
- logSpace: bounds (`logSpace_mono`, `logSpace_le_succ`, `logSpace_le_size_succ`, `logSpace_le_ell`,
  `logSpace_le_lgv`, `logSpace_le_of_le_mul_two_pow`); no equation. Note `logSpace_le_succ`
  (Machines/ARMWidth.lean:51) makes logSpace_eq unnecessary for the SCP route (see 02, F1).
- transduce: `transduce_append`, the machine lemmas (`transducer_run`, `transducerTM_computes`,
  `polyTimeComputable_transduce`) and the two copying-state instances. `transduce_append` yields
  `transduce δ o q [] = []` indirectly (from `t = t ++ t`), but no lemma evaluates
  `transduce δ o q [b]`, so the cons case is unreachable without A1.
Conclusion: the five gaps are real at the tip and on #161, #162 and #135.
