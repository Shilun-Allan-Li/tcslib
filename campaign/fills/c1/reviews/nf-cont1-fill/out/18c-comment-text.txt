3292: rewinding A and clearing B between sweeps. Total `O_N((k+1)·(t+1)) =
3293: C·(t+1)` — the `k+1` covers the zero-work-tape case, where the display
3294: guess and the input sweep remain.
3295: Forward transfer: a genuine accepting run of length `t` yields the accepting
3296: display, guessed and verified within `C·(t+1)`. Backward: a verified display
3297: **is** a genuine run (the sweeps force consistency), so an accepting branch
3298: of the simulator exhibits an accepting branch of `N` at some length —
3299: unbounded on purpose: runaway guessing branches never accept, and no
3300: all-branch-halting claim is made for the simulator. State-space finiteness
3301: packages as `Fin` by the relabeling `Turing.NDTM.relabelState` (the
3302: nondeterministic mirror of `Turing.MultiTapeTM.relabelState`, as in the
3303: `exists_codeTM` precedent). Fill obligations, named: the display format and
3304: its on-the-fly control check; the `k + 1` verification sweeps; the two
3305: transfer directions; the relabeling packaging.
3307: **Implementation.** The display refines the sketch: one record of `2k + 4`
3308: bits per step of `N`, holding the step's choice bit, the claimed input and
3309: work-tape reads (two bits each: `00` blank, `10` false, `11` true) and an end
3310: flag, set on the last record only. States and actions are not guessed: the
3311: verifier recomputes them from `N`'s table in its finite control. The guess
3312: runs under the controller-stopped host `Turing.guessCtlRunTM` with a trivial
3313: setup; its controller stops at the first record boundary after a set end
3314: flag. The verifier is deterministic and silent: a control sweep (a fold by
3315: `Turing.scanTM`) checks the recomputed states, the end flags and the output,
3316: and replay sweeps check the claimed reads against the input and, on the
3317: second work tape, against each replayed work tape, which `Turing.skipTM`,
3318: `Turing.skipBackTM` and `Turing.clearTM` then erase.
