then is at most `s`, `SU` on `pairEncode (Nat.bits s) (pairEncode α x)` halts at some time with
that same output and space at most **`Cα*s`**, with no input-length addend.

the actually published `Turing.CodeTM.toFinTM_eq`, not foreign-definition reduction.
