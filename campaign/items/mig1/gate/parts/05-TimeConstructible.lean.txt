/-- **The binary length counter** (landing: `Turing.FinTM.lengthBitsTM`, made public in place
at `ClassP/TimeConstructible.lean:130` as `_root_.Turing.FinTM.lengthBitsTM`, body unchanged;
today the private `Complexity.counterTM`, copied into Catalog as `f2_counterTM`). A
one-work-tape machine with four states (count `0`, carry `1`, rewind `2`, emit `3`) that keeps a
little-endian binary counter on its work tape, increments it once per input symbol, and on the
blank after the input emits the counter's bits and halts, so that it computes
`x ↦ Nat.bits |x|`. [AB09, §1.3, the examples of time-constructible functions] It is declared
under its `Turing.FinTM` name so that MIG-9 can move it into `Build/` without a rename. -/
-- @@verbatim TimeConstructible TC --public --nodoc counterTM

/-- **The length counter's time** (landing: `Turing.FinTM.lengthBitsTM_computes`, new in
`TimeConstructible` before `timeConstructible_id`; statement verbatim from Catalog's private
`f2_counter_computes`). The counter computes `x ↦ Nat.bits |x|` within `5·(n + 1)` steps.
[AB09, §1.3]

**Proof sketch.** The body of today's `timeConstructible_id` (`TimeConstructible.lean:422`),
which `f2_counter_computes` copies: with twice the number of `true` counter bits as potential,
elapsed time plus potential is at most `4n` after `n` increments (the private `counter_count`);
entering emission and the final halt add two steps, and the emitted word, of length at most
`n`, costs one step per bit. -/
-- @@verbatim Catalog CounterTC --public --nodoc --stmt f2_counter_computes

/-- **The length counter's space** (landing: `Turing.FinTM.lengthBitsTM_spaceUsed`, new in
`TimeConstructible`; statement verbatim from Catalog's private `f2_counter_space`, whose proof
moves with `f2_counter_count_space` and `f2_counter_heads`). At every time, the counter's work
tape has visited at most `5·(Nat.size |x| + 1)` cells: logarithmic space.

**Proof sketch.** Catalog's `f2_counter_space`: every counting round returns the head to the
origin, a carry walks at most the counter's binary width, which never exceeds `Nat.size |x|`,
and emission walks the stored word once, so at every time the head stays in an interval of at
most `5·(Nat.size |x| + 1)` cells (the head ledger `f2_counter_heads`, from the increment
ledger `f2_counter_count_space`). -/
-- @@verbatim Catalog CounterTC --public --nodoc --stmt f2_counter_space

/-- **Row** `Complexity.timeConstructible_id`, statement unchanged. -/
theorem MIG1Gate.row_timeConstructible_id : type_of% @Complexity.timeConstructible_id :=
  ⟨fun n => le_refl n, 5, by decide, FinTM.lengthBitsTM, FinTM.lengthBitsTM_computes⟩
