# MIG-1 statement gate: named witnesses, their spec theorems, the rows as corollaries

**Status: the statement-gate unit of backlog row MIG-1; not a library file.** It elaborates
against the campaign snapshot, in which today's rows are public and today's witnesses private.
The unit has eight parts, one per home file, each opened by a section comment beginning `════`
and by `-- @landing` and `-- @imports` lines. Landing is a set of edits to the existing home
modules, not a new module:

* **Witness definitions.** A witness that is today a private definition of its home file
  (`idTM`, `constTM`, `ifEqTM`, `condTM`, `timedCondTM`, `Turing.pairDiagTM`, `lengthBitsTM`,
  `prependTM`, `incFixedTM`, `pairValidTM`, `pairExtractTM`, `unaryTokenTM`, `appendBitTM`,
  `loopHostTM`, `callTM`) lands by an asserted scripted rename **in place**: the `private`
  keyword goes, the name changes by the rename map of the pack, the body is unchanged, and the
  docstring becomes the one given here. Every other witness (`toCodeTM`, `polyUnaryTM`,
  `polyBitsTM`, `pairLenCheckTM`, `stripLastTM`, `pairMapSndTM`, `splitSolveTM`,
  `splitSolveWithTM`, `emitLoopTM`, `callEntry`, `callExit`) is a new definition whose body is
  today's witness term, from the row proof named in its docstring. `pairMapSndTM` alone lands
  at the fill stage, with the forwarding-controller family that moves from Catalog. The bodies
  here are the source bodies verbatim modulo the rename map
  (`audits/logs/mig1-statements-identity.log`).
* **Gate shims** (blocks between `-- @shim begin` and `-- @shim end`) are verbatim copies of
  private definitions that a witness body uses. They exist only because a private declaration
  cannot be named from another module; on landing they are dropped, since the originals are
  already in the home file (or, for the forwarding controller, move there with their family).
  They are checked verbatim by the same script.
* **Spec theorems and supporting lemmas** are new public statements, inserted in the home file
  before the first row they serve. A spec whose proof needs a private lemma of its home file is
  `sorry` here, under a proof sketch that names the existing proof it comes from; a spec whose
  proof needs only public declarations is proved here, and lands with that proof.
* **Row corollaries** `MIG1Gate.row_X` are stated as `type_of% @X`, the landed row's own type,
  so this unit elaborates only if every row keeps its statement while becoming a corollary of
  the specs. They are not landed in the statement PR: at the fill stage each row's proof becomes
  the proof given here, and the row's statement, name and home stay as they are. The other
  `MIG1Gate` declarations are evidence too: the two head lemmas that move from Catalog to
  Simulation, and the two retired canonical rows re-derived as their consumers will be.

The spec theorems use the library's existing style (`ComputesInTime`, `ComputesFunInTime`,
`spaceUsed` from `initCfg`, `Cfg.ofWords` seams), not `Turing.Contract` instances (user decision
of 2026-10-13; `audits/evidence/mig1/MIG1-design.md` §2). The decision rests on the shape of the
facts (whole-machine runs from `initCfg`, judged by the output, with no exact endpoint) and on the
order of work (a contract needs a named machine, so named witnesses come first, for MIG-11 and
MIG-17); the layering obstacle of design §2, reason 1, lasts only until MIG-0b splits
`Build/Contract.lean` into a core that does not import Catalog.

## Main definitions

* Composition: `idTM`, `constTM`, `ifEqTM`, `condTM`. Wrappers: `timedCondTM`.
  Encoding: `Turing.pairDiagTM`, `toCodeTM`. TimeConstructible: `lengthBitsTM`.
* Loop: `loopHostTM`, `emitLoopTM`, `callTM`, `callEntry`, `callExit`.
* Primitives: `prependTM`, `incFixedTM`, `pairValidTM`, `pairExtractTM`, `polyUnaryTM`,
  `polyBitsTM`, `pairLenCheckTM`, `stripLastTM`, `pairMapSndTM`, `splitSolveTM`,
  `splitSolveWithTM`, `unaryTokenTM`, `appendBitTM`.

## Main results

* The generic run lemmas `Turing.MultiTapeTM.abs_workTapePos_runFrom_sub_le` and
  `Turing.FinTM.ComputesInTime.spaceUsed_le` (proved), and the canonical-word endpoint lemmas
  `Turing.Cfg.ofWords_copy_endpoint`, `Turing.Cfg.ofWords_transfer_endpoint` (proved).
* One time spec per witness, a space spec for the witnesses with their own space argument,
  and the configuration-level contracts of the loop host and the clean call.
* The 53 rows, `Complexity.timeConstructible_id` and `Turing.FinTM.redirectTM_spaceUsedByTape`
  as `MIG1Gate.row_*` corollaries.
