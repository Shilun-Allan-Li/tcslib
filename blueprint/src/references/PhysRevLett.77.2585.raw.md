Clarendon Laboratory, Department of Physics, University of Oxford, Parks Road, Oxford OX1 3PU, United Kingdom

We show how procedures which can correct phase and amplitude errors are in themselves sufﬁcient
to correct errors due to quantum entanglement, generalizing earlier results. We specify general criteria
for quantum error correction, introduce quantum versions of the Hamming and the Gilbert-Varshamov
[S0031-9007(96)01212-4]
bounds, and comment on the practical implementations of quantum codes.
