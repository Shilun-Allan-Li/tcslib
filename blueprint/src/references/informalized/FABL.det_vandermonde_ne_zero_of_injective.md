# Nonvanishing of the Vandermonde Determinant

**Claim.** For pairwise distinct elements of a field, the determinant of their Vandermonde matrix is nonzero.

**Proof.** The Vandermonde determinant criterion states that the determinant of the Vandermonde matrix is nonzero exactly when the entries of its defining tuple are pairwise distinct. Applying the forward implication from injectivity of the tuple gives the result.
