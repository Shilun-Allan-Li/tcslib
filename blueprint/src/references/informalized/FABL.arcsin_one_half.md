# Arcsine of One Half

**Claim.** The principal inverse sine of $1/2$ is $\pi/6$.

**Proof.** The sine of $\pi/6$ is $1/2$, and $\pi/6$ lies in the range $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$ on which the principal inverse sine inverts sine. Therefore $\arcsin(1/2)=\pi/6$.
