# Changing a Different Coordinate

**Claim.** Replacing coordinate $i$ of a sign vector by a sign $b$ leaves coordinate $j$ unchanged whenever $j\ne i$.

**Proof.** The updated vector agrees with the original at every coordinate other than $i$. Since $j\ne i$, its value at $j$ is therefore $x_j$.
