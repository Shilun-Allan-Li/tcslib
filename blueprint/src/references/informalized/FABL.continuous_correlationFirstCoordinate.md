# Continuity of the first coordinate

**Claim.** On the real plane equipped with the $2$-norm, taking a point to its first real coordinate is continuous.

**Proof.** The map that takes a point of the $2$-norm plane to its underlying pair is continuous, and projection onto the first factor of $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$ is continuous. Their composition is the first-coordinate map, hence is continuous.
