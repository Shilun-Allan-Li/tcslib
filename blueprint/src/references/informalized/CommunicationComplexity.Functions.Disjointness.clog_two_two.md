# Ceiling Logarithm of Two

**Claim.** The base-two ceiling logarithm of $2$ equals $1$.

**Proof.** The characterization of the ceiling logarithm at one gives $\operatorname{clog}(b,n)=1$ when $1<b\le n$. For $b=n=2$, both inequalities hold with equality, so $\operatorname{clog}(2,2)=1$.
