# Restricted depth bounded by its maximum

**Claim.** For each member of the finite family, its restricted decision-tree depth is at most the maximum restricted depth over the family.

**Proof.** The maximum is defined as the supremum of the restricted depths over all indices. The chosen index belongs to the full index set, so its value is at most that supremum.
