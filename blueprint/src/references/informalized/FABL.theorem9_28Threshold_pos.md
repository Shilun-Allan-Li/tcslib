# Positivity of the Influence Threshold

**Claim.** If $\varepsilon>0$ and the total influence is positive, then $\frac{\varepsilon^2}{I(f)^2}(1/9)^k$ is positive for every natural number $k$.

**Proof.** The square $\varepsilon^2$ is positive because $\varepsilon>0$, and the squared total influence is positive because the total influence is positive. The base $1/9$ is positive, so its $k$th power is positive for every natural number $k$. A quotient of positive numbers, multiplied by a positive number, remains positive. This is precisely the stated threshold.
