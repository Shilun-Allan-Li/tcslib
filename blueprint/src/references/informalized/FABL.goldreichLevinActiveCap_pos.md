# Positivity of the Active Cap

**Claim.** For every rational threshold $\tau$ with $0<\tau\le 1$, the active cap $\lceil 4/\tau^2\rceil$ is positive.

**Proof.** By definition, the active cap is the natural-number ceiling of $4/\tau^2$. Since $4>0$ and $\tau>0$, the quotient $4/\tau^2$ is positive. The ceiling of a positive rational is a positive natural number, giving the claim.
