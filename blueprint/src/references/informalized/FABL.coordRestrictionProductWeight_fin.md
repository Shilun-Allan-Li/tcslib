# Finite product weight on coordinates

**Claim.** The product of the coordinate weights over $\mathrm{Fin}(n)$ equals the restriction assignment weight: free coordinates contribute $\delta$, while each fixed coordinate contributes $(1-\delta)/2$.

**Proof.** Both sides are defined as the product, over all coordinates in $\mathrm{Fin}(n)$, of the weight assigned to the restriction at that coordinate. Unfolding the two definitions therefore gives identical expressions.
