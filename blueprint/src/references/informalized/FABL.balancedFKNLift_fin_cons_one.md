# Balanced Lift at Positive Leading Sign

**Claim.** For the balanced lift of $f$, evaluation at the vector with leading coordinate $1$ and tail $x$ is $f(x)$.

**Proof.** At $z=(1,x)$, the leading factor is $1$, and multiplying each tail coordinate by it leaves the tail equal to $x$. Thus the lift evaluates to $1\cdot f(x)=f(x)$.
