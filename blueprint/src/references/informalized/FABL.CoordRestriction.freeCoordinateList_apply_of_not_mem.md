# Unlisted Coordinates Are Unchanged

**Claim.** For any coordinate $i$ not in $L$, the restriction obtained by making every coordinate in $L$ free agrees with $\eta$ at $i$.

**Proof.** Since $i$ does not belong to $L$, the modification does not replace its state. Its value at $i$ is therefore the original value $\eta(i)$.
