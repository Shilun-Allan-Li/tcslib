# Power of a negative indeterminate

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, $(-X)^m=(-1)^mX^m$ in $\mathbb R[X]$.

**Proof.** The power of a negated element satisfies $(-X)^m=(-1)^mX^m$. Viewing the real scalar $(-1)^m$ as a constant polynomial expresses the right-hand side as the product of that constant polynomial with $X^m$, which is the stated identity.
