# Adjacency of matched partners

**Claim.** For every perfect matching of the complete graph on $2m$ vertices and every vertex $v$, its matched partner is adjacent to $v$ in the matching.

**Proof.** The characterization of a perfect matching says that for each vertex $v$, there is a unique vertex joined to it by an edge, and the chosen partner is that vertex. In particular, the chosen partner is adjacent to $v$.
