# Square of the Main Influence Term

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, the square of $\sqrt{2/(\pi(2m+1))}$ equals $2/(\pi(2m+1))$.

**Proof.** By definition, the main term is the square root of $2/(\pi(2m+1))$. Squaring a square root recovers its radicand, since that radicand is nonnegative.
