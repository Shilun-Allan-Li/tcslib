# Moment gap vanishes at zero

**Claim.** For a finite nonempty set $\Omega$, the hypercontractive moment gap is zero at $t=0$, for all real $p,q,\rho$ and all $X\colon\Omega\to\mathbb R$.

**Proof.** At $t=0$, both affine factors are identically $1$. Their averages, at any real moment orders, are therefore $1$. The two powers defining the gap are both $1$, and their difference is zero.
