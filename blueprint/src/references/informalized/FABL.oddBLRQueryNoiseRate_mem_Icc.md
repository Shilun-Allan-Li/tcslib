# Noise Rate Lies in the Unit Interval

**Claim.** If $0<\delta<1/2$, then the noise rate $2\delta/3$ lies in $[0,1]$.

**Proof.** The lower bound follows from $\delta>0$, which gives $2\delta/3>0$. For the upper bound, $\delta<1/2$ implies $2\delta/3<1/3<1$. Hence the rate belongs to $[0,1]$.
