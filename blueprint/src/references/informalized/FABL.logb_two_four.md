# Base-Two Logarithm of Four

**Claim.** The base-two logarithm of $4$ equals $2$.

**Proof.** Since $4=2^2$ and the base-two logarithm of $2$ is $1$, the logarithm power rule gives $\log_2 4=2\log_2 2=2$.
