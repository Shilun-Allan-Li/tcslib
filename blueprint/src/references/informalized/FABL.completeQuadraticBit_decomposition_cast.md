# Transporting a quadratic decomposition

**Claim.** If $m=n$, any quadratic decomposition on $\mathbb{F}_2^m$ of the stated form, with a linearly independent combined family of linear functionals, gives a decomposition with the same properties on $\mathbb{F}_2^n$.

**Proof.** Since $m=n$, replace $n$ by $m$ in the desired conclusion. The resulting existential statement is exactly the assumed decomposition, including its linear independence and identity for every vector, so the assumption itself supplies the witnesses.
