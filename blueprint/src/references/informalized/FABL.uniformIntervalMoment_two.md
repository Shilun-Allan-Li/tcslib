# Second uniform interval moment

**Claim.** The second moment of the uniform distribution on $[-1,1]$, defined by $\frac12\int_{-1}^{1}x^2\,dx$, is $\frac13$.

**Proof.** The power rule gives $\int_{-1}^{1}x^2\,dx=[x^3/3]_{-1}^{1}=1/3-(-1/3)=2/3$. Multiplying by $1/2$ yields $1/3$.
