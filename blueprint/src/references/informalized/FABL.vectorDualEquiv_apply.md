# Evaluation of the Vector Dual Map

**Claim.** The linear functional corresponding to $\gamma$ under the standard vector-to-dual equivalence, evaluated at $x$, equals the dot product of $\gamma$ and $x$ over $\mathbb F_2$.

**Proof.** By definition, the standard vector-to-dual equivalence sends a vector to the linear functional given by dot product with that vector. Evaluating the resulting functional at $x$ is therefore exactly the dot product of $\gamma$ and $x$, which is the specified $\mathbb F_2$-valued expression.
