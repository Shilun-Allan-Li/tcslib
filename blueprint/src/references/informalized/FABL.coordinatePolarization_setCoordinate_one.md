# Polarization at the positive coordinate

**Claim.** For a sign-valued function $f$, coordinate $i$, and point $x$, its coordinate polarization evaluated after setting coordinate $i$ to $1$ is the maximum of the values of $f$ at the two points obtained by setting coordinate $i$ to $1$ and to $-1$.

**Proof.** At the point obtained by setting coordinate $i$ to $1$, the value of coordinate $i$ is $1$. The definition of coordinate polarization therefore selects the maximum branch, with arguments given by the values of $f$ at the two indicated points.
