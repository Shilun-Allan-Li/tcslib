# Tail Bound for Conditional Bit Distance

**Claim.** If $\gamma>0$ and the mean distance is at most $\gamma^2$ under the conditioned hard distribution, then the measure of samples where the distance exceeds $\gamma$ is at most $\gamma$.

**Proof.** Let $f(\omega)$ be the distance at the value of $Z$ generated by $\omega$, and let $\mu$ be the conditioned law, which is a probability measure (\texttt{disjointSpecialYFalseMeasure_isProbabilityMeasure}). Markov’s inequality for the nonnegative function $f$ gives $\gamma\,\mu(\{\omega:\gamma\leq f(\omega)\})\leq\int f\,d\mu$; nonnegativity follows from (\texttt{xDistance_nonneg}). The event where $f>\gamma$ is contained in the event where $f\geq\gamma$, so its measure is no larger. Combining Markov’s inequality with the assumed bound yields $\gamma\,\mu(\{f\geq\gamma\})\leq\gamma^2$. Since $\gamma>0$ and measures are nonnegative, division by $\gamma$ shows that this latter event has measure at most $\gamma$. The same bound follows for the strict tail event.
