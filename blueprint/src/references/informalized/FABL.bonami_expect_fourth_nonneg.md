# Nonnegative fourth moment

**Claim.** For every real-valued function on the $n$-dimensional sign cube, the uniform expectation of its fourth power is nonnegative.

**Proof.** At each point $x$ of the sign cube, $f(x)^4$ is nonnegative, since an even power of a real number is nonnegative. The uniform expectation is a finite weighted average with nonnegative weights, so it is nonnegative as well.
