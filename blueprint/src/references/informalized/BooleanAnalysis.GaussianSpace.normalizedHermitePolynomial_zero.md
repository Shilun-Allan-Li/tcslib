# Zeroth normalized Hermite polynomial

**Claim.** The normalized Hermite polynomial of degree zero is the constant polynomial $1$.

**Proof.** At degree zero, the Hermite polynomial and the normalizing factor are both $1$: the former is the constant polynomial $1$, and $0!=1$. Thus the definition gives the constant polynomial $1$.
