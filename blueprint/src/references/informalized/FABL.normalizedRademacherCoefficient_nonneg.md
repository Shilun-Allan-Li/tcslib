# Nonnegative Normalized Coefficient

**Claim.** For every natural number $n$, the coefficient $(\sqrt n)^{-1}$ is nonnegative, with the reciprocal interpreted as zero when $n=0$.

**Proof.** The square root of $n$ is nonnegative. The reciprocal of any nonnegative real is nonnegative, including at zero under the totalized reciprocal convention. Therefore $(\sqrt n)^{-1}\ge0$.
