# Inner Product as an Expectation

**Claim.** For a finite set, the probability inner product of $f$ and $g$ equals the expectation of their pointwise product under $\pi$.

**Proof.** The probability inner product is defined to be the expectation of the pointwise product: its defining expression is $\mathbb E_\pi[x\mapsto f(x)g(x)]$. Thus the two sides are equal by definition.
