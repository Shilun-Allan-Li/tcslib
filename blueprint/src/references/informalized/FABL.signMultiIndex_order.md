# Order of a Sign Multi-Index

**Claim.** The order of a sign multi-index equals the cardinality of its support.

**Proof.** By definition, the order of a multi-index at zero is the cardinality of the set of coordinates where its value differs from zero. The sign multi-index support is that same set, so their cardinalities agree.
