# Statistic Records the Special Coordinate

**Claim.** If the statistic of a hard sample equals $z$, then the sample's special coordinate equals the special-coordinate component of $z$.

**Proof.** Take the special-coordinate component on both sides of the equality between the sample's statistic and $z$. By the definition of the statistic, its special-coordinate component is the sample's special coordinate. The resulting equality is the claim.
