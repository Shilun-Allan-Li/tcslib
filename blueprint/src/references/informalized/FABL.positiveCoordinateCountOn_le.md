# Positive Count on a Subset

**Claim.** For a subset $S$ of the coordinates and a sign vector $x$, the number of coordinates in $S$ where $x$ equals $1$ is at most $|S|$.

**Proof.** The coordinates in $S$ where $x$ equals $1$ form a subset of $S$. The cardinality of a subset of a finite set is at most the cardinality of that set, giving the result.
