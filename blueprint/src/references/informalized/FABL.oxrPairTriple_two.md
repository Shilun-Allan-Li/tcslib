# Third Coordinate of the Pair Triple

**Claim.** For any pair of signs $(a,b)$, the third coordinate of the triple $(a,b,-b)$ is $-b$.

**Proof.** By the definition of the triple as the function with entries $(a,b,-b)$, evaluating at the third coordinate returns $-b$.
