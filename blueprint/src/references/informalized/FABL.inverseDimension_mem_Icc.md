# Reciprocal Dimension in the Unit Interval

**Claim.** For every positive natural number $m$, the reciprocal $1/m$ belongs to $[0,1]$.

**Proof.** Positivity of $m$ as a real number gives $1/m>0$. Also, a positive natural number is at least $1$, so $1/m\leq 1$. These two inequalities place the reciprocal in $[0,1]$.
