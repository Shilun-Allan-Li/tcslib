# Friedgut Degree at One Eighth

**Claim.** At parameter $1/8$, the Friedgut degree of a Boolean function is the least integer greater than or equal to eight times its total influence.

**Proof.** By definition, the degree is the natural ceiling of total influence divided by the parameter. Substituting $1/8$ makes this quotient $8$ times the total influence, since division by $1/8$ is multiplication by $8$. The two natural ceilings are consequently equal.
