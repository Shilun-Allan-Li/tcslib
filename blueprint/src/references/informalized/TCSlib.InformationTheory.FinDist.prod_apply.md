# Product Distribution Probability

**Claim.** For distributions $p$ on $\alpha$ and $q$ on $\beta$, their product distribution assigns probability $p(a)q(b)$ to each pair $(a,b)$.

**Proof.** The product distribution is defined pointwise by multiplying the probabilities of the two coordinates. Evaluating its probability function at $(a,b)$ gives $p(a)q(b)$ directly.
