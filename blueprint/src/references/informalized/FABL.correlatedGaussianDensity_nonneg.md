# Nonnegativity of the Correlated Gaussian Density

**Claim.** For every $0<\rho<1$ and all real $z_1,z_2$, the correlated Gaussian density at $(z_1,z_2)$ is nonnegative.

**Proof.** By definition, the correlated Gaussian density is the product of two Gaussian densities: the standard density at $z_1$ and the conditional density at $z_2$. Each Gaussian density is nonnegative, so their product is nonnegative.
