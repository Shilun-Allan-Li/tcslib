# Restricted frequency membership

**Claim.** For $i\in J$, membership in the subset of $J$ consisting of coordinates in $U$ is equivalent to the underlying coordinate of $i$ belonging to $U$.

**Proof.** This subset is defined by filtering $J$ for elements whose underlying coordinate lies in $U$. The membership condition is therefore exactly that the underlying coordinate of $i$ belongs to $U$.
