# Dual Fourier support membership

**Claim.** A linear functional belongs to the dual Fourier support of $f$ exactly when its dual Fourier coefficient is nonzero.

**Proof.** The dual Fourier support is defined by filtering the finite set of linear functionals to retain exactly those whose dual Fourier coefficient is nonzero. Thus membership in the support is equivalent to that nonvanishing condition.
