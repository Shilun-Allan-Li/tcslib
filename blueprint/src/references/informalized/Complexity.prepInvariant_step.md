# Preservation of the preparation invariant

**Claim.** If a live schedule configuration satisfies the preparation invariant, then after one schedule step the invariant holds for the visit's updated register, the data tapes after the prescribed writes at the guide head's current position, and the output list extended by the optional output symbol.

**Proof.** Unfold the one-step update and split on the current phase. In the initial and clock phases, the controller establishes or preserves the unary origin marker and head positions while the data contents remain blank; the clock transition may change the simulated state, but does not alter these preparation conditions. During reset, the input head scans back to the left boundary, and the blank-input characterization (`Complexity.inputSymbol_blank`) gives the boundary case needed to show the updated input position satisfies the reset-scan condition. During copying, the input head's position identifies the next bit to copy: `Complexity.copy_input_read` identifies the read bit, and `Complexity.copy_input_payload` identifies the payload written for it. At the left end, `Complexity.copyDataWrite_left` establishes the initial copied-content description; at each subsequent position, `Complexity.copyDataWrite_next` advances that description by one cell. The end-of-input and ordinary-bit cases then give the required copied-prefix invariant. In the return, budget, append, borrow, and unary phases, the data content is already full and stays so; when the unary head moves onto a unit, `Complexity.unaryOrigin_update` ensures the unique origin marker remains at zero. In the layout phases, the full-content and sentinel clauses are preserved as the controller lays out the guide tape. In the center and macro phases, the stated phase conditions follow from the guide and unary markers; when the macro check executes the simulated transition, its data update is exactly the visit's prescribed write. Finally, in the seek, forward, backward, and return phases, the invariant asks only for the sentinel and a lower bound on the unary head, and the controller's moves preserve those conditions.
