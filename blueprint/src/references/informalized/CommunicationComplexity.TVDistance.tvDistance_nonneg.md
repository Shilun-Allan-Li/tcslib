# Nonnegativity of Total Variation Distance

**Claim.** The total variation distance between probability measures on a measurable space is nonnegative.

**Proof.** By definition, total variation distance is one half times the real mass of a measure on the whole space. That mass is nonnegative, and the factor one half is nonnegative, so their product is nonnegative.
