# Reciprocal Gaussian Mass Bound

**Claim.** For every natural number $q$, if $\gamma_q=1/(q+2)$, then $1<1/\gamma_q$.

**Proof.** The mass $\gamma_q$ is positive and strictly less than $1$, since $q+2>1$. For a positive denominator, the inequality $1<1/\gamma_q$ is equivalent to $\gamma_q<1$.
