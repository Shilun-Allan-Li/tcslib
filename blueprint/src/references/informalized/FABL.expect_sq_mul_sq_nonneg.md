# Nonnegativity of a Product of Squares

**Claim.** For any real-valued functions $d$ and $e$ on the sign cube, the uniform expectation of $d(x)^2e(x)^2$ is nonnegative.

**Proof.** Write the uniform expectation as the sum of the values $d(x)^2e(x)^2$ divided by the positive cardinality of the cube. Each summand is nonnegative because it is a product of squares, so the expectation is nonnegative.
