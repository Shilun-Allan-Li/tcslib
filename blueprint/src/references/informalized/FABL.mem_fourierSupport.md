# Fourier support membership

**Claim.** A finite coordinate set belongs to the Fourier support of a real-valued function on the sign cube exactly when its Fourier coefficient is nonzero.

**Proof.** By definition, the Fourier support consists of all finite coordinate sets whose Fourier coefficient is nonzero. Thus membership in the support is equivalent to that condition.
