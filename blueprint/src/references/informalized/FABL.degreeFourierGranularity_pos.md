# Positivity of Fourier granularity

**Claim.** For every natural number $k$, the Fourier granularity at level $k$ is positive.

**Proof.** By definition, the granularity is $2(1/2)^k$. Both factors are positive: $2>0$, and every natural power of the positive number $1/2$ is positive. Their product is therefore positive.
