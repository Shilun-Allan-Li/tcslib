# Jordan decomposition evaluation formula

**Claim.** For a signed measure $s$ and any measurable set $S$, its value on $S$ is the positive-part mass of $S$ minus the negative-part mass of $S$.

**Proof.** The Jordan decomposition expresses $s$ as the signed measure associated to the difference of its positive and negative parts. Evaluating that difference on the measurable set $S$ gives the positive-part mass minus the negative-part mass, as claimed.
