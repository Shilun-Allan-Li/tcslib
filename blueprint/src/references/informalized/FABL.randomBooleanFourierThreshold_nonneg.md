# Nonnegative Fourier threshold

**Claim.** For every natural number $n$, the quantity $2\sqrt{n}\,2^{-n/2}$ is nonnegative.

**Proof.** The threshold is a product of three nonnegative factors: $2>0$, the square root $\sqrt n\ge 0$, and the real power $2^{-n/2}\ge 0$. The product is therefore nonnegative.
