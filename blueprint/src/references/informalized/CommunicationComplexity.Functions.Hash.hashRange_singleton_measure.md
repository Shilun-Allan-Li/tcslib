# Singleton measure in a finite range

**Claim.** Under the uniform volume measure on $\mathrm{Fin}\,k$, each singleton has measure $1/k$.

**Proof.** The volume measure is the uniform measure on the whole finite range. The uniform measure of the whole range assigns equal mass to each of its $k$ elements, with total mass $1$; consequently each singleton has mass $1/k$.
