# Adjacency of the Matching Partner

**Claim.** For every vertex in a perfect matching of the complete $q$-partite graph with $k$ vertices in each part, its matching partner is adjacent to it in the matching graph.

**Proof.** The perfect-matching property says that each vertex has a unique matching neighbor, and the partner map is defined to select that neighbor. Its defining existence statement therefore directly supplies the required adjacency.
