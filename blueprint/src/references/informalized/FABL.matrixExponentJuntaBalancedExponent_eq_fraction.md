# Balanced Exponent as a Fraction

**Claim.** For a positive rational matrix exponent $\omega$ and a natural number $k$, the balanced exponent equals $\bigl(\omega/(\omega+1)\bigr)k$.

**Proof.** Unfold the definition of the balanced exponent, which is $\omega k/(\omega+1)$. Associativity and commutativity of rational multiplication identify this with $(\omega/(\omega+1))k$, proving the identity.
