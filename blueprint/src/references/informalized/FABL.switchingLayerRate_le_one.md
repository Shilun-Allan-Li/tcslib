# Switching layer rate upper bound

**Claim.** For every positive integer $w$, the switching layer rate $1/(10w)$ is at most $1$.

**Proof.** Since $w$ is a positive integer, its real value is at least $1$. Hence $10w\ge1>0$. Taking reciprocals reverses the inequality between these positive denominators, giving $1/(10w)\le1$.
