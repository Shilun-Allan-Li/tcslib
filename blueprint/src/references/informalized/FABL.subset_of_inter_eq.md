# Subset of an Intersection

**Claim.** If $U\cap J=S$, then $S\subseteq J$.

**Proof.** Take any $x\in S$. The equality $U\cap J=S$ implies $x\in U\cap J$, and membership in an intersection gives $x\in J$. Thus every element of $S$ belongs to $J$.
