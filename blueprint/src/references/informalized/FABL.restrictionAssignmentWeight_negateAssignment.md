# Negation invariance of ordinary assignment weight

**Claim.** Exchanging the two fixed states in every coordinate, while leaving free states unchanged, preserves the ordinary assignment weight.

**Proof.** Compare the product factors at each coordinate. A free state remains free, and its weight remains $1/2$. The two fixed states are exchanged, but each has weight $1/4$. Therefore every factor is unchanged, and the products are equal.
