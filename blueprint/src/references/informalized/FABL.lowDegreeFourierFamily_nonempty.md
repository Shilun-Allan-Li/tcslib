# Nonempty Low-Degree Family

**Claim.** For all nonnegative integers $n$ and $k$, the family of subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ of size at most $k$ is nonempty.

**Proof.** The empty set has size zero, hence at most $k$. It therefore belongs to the low-degree family, proving nonemptiness.
