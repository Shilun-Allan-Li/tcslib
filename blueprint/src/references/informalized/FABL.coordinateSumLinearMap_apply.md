# Coordinate Sum Evaluation

**Claim.** For every function $x$ from the $m$ coordinates to $\mathbb{F}_2$, the coordinate-sum linear map evaluates to $\sum_i x(i)$.

**Proof.** By definition, the linear map sends $x$ to the sum of its coordinate values. Thus its evaluation at $x$ is exactly $\sum_i x(i)$.
