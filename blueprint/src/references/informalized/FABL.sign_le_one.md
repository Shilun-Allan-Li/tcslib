# A Sign Is at Most One

**Claim.** Every integer sign $s\in\{-1,1\}$ satisfies $s\le 1$.

**Proof.** A sign is either $1$ or $-1$. The first value is at most $1$ by equality, and the second is at most $1$ by the usual ordering of the integers.
