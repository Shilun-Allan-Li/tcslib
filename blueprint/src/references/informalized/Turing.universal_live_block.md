# One Live Interpreter Step Within the Block Bound

**Claim.** For a live source configuration and a table cursor at most the serialization length, the interpreter simulates one machine step in a positive number of steps bounded by $3L+5(N+1)+20$, ending with a cursor still within the table.

**Proof.** Since the source is live, write its state as $q$. Let $a$ be the machine's action on the current input and work-tape symbols. The table-decomposition lemma (Turing.universal_lookup_parts) separates the serialization into its header, the records for earlier state groups, the records preceding the read-pair's action in state $q$, that action's record, and a suffix. Starting from the represented source configuration, the interpreter first rewinds to the table start and counts the paired state-count bits; the run facts for rewinding and counting (Turing.universal_table_rewind, Turing.universal_count_run), followed by the initial-state skip lemma (Turing.universal_initial_skip), position it at the record groups. The selection lemma (Turing.universal_select) passes the earlier groups and records, reaching the record for $a$. The record-shape lemma (Turing.universal_record_shape) expresses that record as eight fixed action bits followed by the unary next-state field and its delimiter. The fixed-field reading lemma (Turing.universal_read_fixed) reads those eight bits, and the next-state preparation lemma (Turing.universal_prepare_next) processes the unary field and establishes the correct next-state tape and cursor. Finally, the interpreter applies the decoded action: the one-step application identity (Turing.universal_apply_record) shows that the represented configuration becomes the machine's actual successor configuration. The interpreter transitions in these stages concatenate by the run-joining law (Turing.universal_run_join); the machine's step is indeed $a$ applied to the source by the definition of the transition function. For the bounds, the duration is the sum of the rewind, count, skip, selection, fixed-read, next-state, and action-application costs. The table decomposition gives the length accounting for those pieces, each state group has nine records, and the next-state field has at most $N+1$ unary ones. Together with $q,q_0\le N$, these yield a positive duration at most $3L+5(N+1)+20$. The computed cursor is a position in the serialized record and its suffix, hence is at most $L.
