# Uniqueness of the Matching Partner

**Claim.** If a vertex $w$ is adjacent to a vertex $v$ in the perfect matching, then $w$ is the matching partner of $v$.

**Proof.** For each vertex, the perfect-matching property supplies a matching neighbor and asserts that every adjacent neighbor equals that chosen one. Applying this uniqueness clause to the given adjacent vertex $w$ proves the claim.
