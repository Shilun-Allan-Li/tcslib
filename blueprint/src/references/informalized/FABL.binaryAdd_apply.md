# Coordinate Formula for Binary Addition

**Claim.** At each coordinate, the binary sum of two vectors is the sum of their values at that coordinate.

**Proof.** By definition, binary addition assigns to each coordinate $i$ the value $a_i+b_i$. Thus evaluating the resulting vector at $i$ gives precisely the stated coordinate formula.
