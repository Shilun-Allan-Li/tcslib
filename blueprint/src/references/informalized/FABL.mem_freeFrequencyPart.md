# Free frequency part membership

**Claim.** For an element of the finite set $J$, its underlying coordinate belongs to the free frequency part of $J$ relative to $U$ exactly when that coordinate lies in $U$.

**Proof.** The free frequency part is defined by filtering all elements of $J$ according to whether their underlying coordinate lies in $U$. Therefore an element belongs to it precisely when its underlying coordinate belongs to $U$.
