# The Zero-Dimensional Inner Product

**Claim.** For the unique Boolean input of length zero, split into two blocks of length zero, the inner product of the blocks over the field with two elements is not $1$.

**Proof.** In dimension zero, each block is the unique function on an empty index set. Its dot product is an empty sum, hence equals $0$ in the field with two elements. Since $0\ne 1$, the inner product cannot equal $1$.
