# Two-variable AND formula

**Claim.** For signs $a,b\in\{-1,1\}$, the real value of the two-variable AND function at $(a,b)$ is $(1+a+b-ab)/2$.

**Proof.** There are four sign pairs. If $a=b=-1$, the AND value is $-1$, and the displayed expression is $-1$. For each of the other three pairs, the AND value is $1$, and substitution into the expression gives $1$. Thus the formula holds in every case.
