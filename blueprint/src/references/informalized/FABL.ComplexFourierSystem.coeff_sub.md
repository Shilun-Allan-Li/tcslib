# Linearity under subtraction

**Claim.** The coefficient of $f-g$ at any basis index is the coefficient of $f$ minus the coefficient of $g$ at that index.

**Proof.** A Fourier coefficient is a coordinate of the function in the chosen basis. Taking a coordinate is linear, so the coordinate of the difference $f-g$ is the difference of the two coordinates.
