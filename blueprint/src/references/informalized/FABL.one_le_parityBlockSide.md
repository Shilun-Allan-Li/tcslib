# Positivity of the Parity Block Side

**Claim.** For all natural numbers $n$ and $h$, the parity block side is at least $1$.

**Proof.** The parity block side is the maximum of $1$ and the natural-number ceiling of the specified real root. A maximum is at least each of its entries, so it is at least $1$.
