# Product Gaussian measure has plane density

**Claim.** The product of two standard Gaussian measures on $\mathbb R$ equals Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb R^2$ weighted by the standard Gaussian plane density.

**Proof.** Each standard Gaussian measure is Lebesgue measure weighted by its one-dimensional Gaussian density. The product of these two weighted measures is the product Lebesgue measure weighted by the product of the two densities. Product Lebesgue measure is Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb R^2$, and that product of densities is precisely the standard Gaussian plane density.
