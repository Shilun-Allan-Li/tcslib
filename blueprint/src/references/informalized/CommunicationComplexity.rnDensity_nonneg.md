# Nonnegative Radon–Nikodym density

**Claim.** For probability measures μ and ν, the real-valued Radon–Nikodym density of μ with respect to ν is nonnegative at every point.

**Proof.** The density at a point is the real coercion of the extended nonnegative real value of the Radon–Nikodym derivative there. The real coercion of any extended nonnegative real is nonnegative, so the claim follows directly.
