# Euler Liar Subgroup Membership

**Claim.** A unit modulo nonzero $N$ belongs to the Euler liar subgroup exactly when its Jacobi symbol, regarded in $\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z$, equals its $(N-1)/2$-th power in that ring.

**Proof.** The subgroup is defined to have as its carrier precisely the units satisfying the displayed Jacobi-symbol equality. Thus membership in the subgroup is equivalent to that equality by the definition of the carrier.
