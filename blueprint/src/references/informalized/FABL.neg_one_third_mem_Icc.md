# Membership in the unit interval

**Claim.** The real number $-1/3$ belongs to the closed interval $[-1,1]$.

**Proof.** Membership in $[-1,1]$ means satisfying both endpoint inequalities. Since $-1=-3/3$ and $1=3/3$, and the denominator $3$ is positive, we have $-1\le -1/3\le 1$. Thus $-1/3$ lies in the interval.
