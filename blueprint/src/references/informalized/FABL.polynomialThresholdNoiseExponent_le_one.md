# Noise Exponent Upper Bound

**Claim.** For every natural number $k$, the exponent $2^{-k}$ is at most $1$.

**Proof.** Since $2\geq 1$, its natural-number power $2^k$ is at least $1$. Taking reciprocals of positive quantities reverses the inequality, giving $(2^k)^{-1}\leq 1$.
