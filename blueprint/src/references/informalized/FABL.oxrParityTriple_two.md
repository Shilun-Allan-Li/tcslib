# Third parity coordinate

**Claim.** For any pair of signs $(a,b)$, the third coordinate of the associated parity triple is $-ab$.

**Proof.** The parity triple is defined by the three-entry vector $(a,b,-ab)$. Its third coordinate is therefore $-ab$ by the definition of the vector.
