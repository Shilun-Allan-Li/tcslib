# Zero total signed difference

**Claim.** The signed measure difference of two probability measures assigns mass zero to the whole space.

**Proof.** Evaluate the signed measure difference on the whole space. The evaluation of a difference of signed measures there is the difference of their total masses. Each probability measure has total mass one, so this difference is $1-1=0$.
