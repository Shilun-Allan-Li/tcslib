# Sum over the Two Signs

**Claim.** For every real-valued function on the two integer units, its sum over those units equals its value at $1$ plus its value at $-1$.

**Proof.** The finite set of integer units consists exactly of $1$ and $-1$. Splitting the sum over this two-element set into its two singleton contributions gives $g(1)+g(-1)$.
