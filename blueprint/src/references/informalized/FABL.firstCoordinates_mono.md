# Monotonicity of initial coordinate sets

**Claim.** If $s\le t$, then among $n$ coordinates the first-$s$ set is contained in the first-$t$ set.

**Proof.** Take any coordinate in the first-$s$ set. Its index is less than $s$, and since $s\le t$, it is also less than $t$. Therefore it belongs to the first-$t$ set.
