# The infinite-exponent uniform norm

**Claim.** For a finite nonempty set $\Omega$ and a function $f:\Omega\to\mathbb R$, the extended-exponent uniform norm of $f$ at exponent $\infty$ equals its supremum norm.

**Proof.** At exponent $\infty$, the condition in the definition of the extended-exponent norm holds, so the definition selects the uniform $L^\infty$ norm directly. Thus the two sides are equal.
