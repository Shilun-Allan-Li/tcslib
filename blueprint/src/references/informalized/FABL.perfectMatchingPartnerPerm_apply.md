# Matching Partner Permutation Evaluation

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, every perfect matching of the complete graph on $2m$ vertices, and every vertex $v$, the associated permutation sends $v$ to its matched partner.

**Proof.** The associated permutation is defined from the involution that sends each vertex to its matching partner. Evaluating that permutation therefore gives the partner of $v$ directly.
