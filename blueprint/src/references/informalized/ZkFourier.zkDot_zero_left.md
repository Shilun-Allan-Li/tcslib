# Zero in the left argument

**Claim.** For any vector $x\in(\mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z})^n$, the dot product of the zero vector with $x$ is zero.

**Proof.** Every summand in the dot product is $0\cdot x_i=0$. Therefore their finite sum is zero.
