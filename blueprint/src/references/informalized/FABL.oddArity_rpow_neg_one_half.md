# Negative Half Power Identity

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, $(2m+1)^{-1/2}=1/\sqrt{2m+1}$.

**Proof.** Since $2m+1>0$, the negative-power rule gives $(2m+1)^{-1/2}=1\big/(2m+1)^{1/2}$. The positive half power is the square root, yielding the claimed reciprocal.
