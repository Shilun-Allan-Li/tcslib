# Mean of a Uniform Sign

**Claim.** A uniform sign has mean zero: the average of its real value over the two signs $-1$ and $1$ is $0$.

**Proof.** Write the uniform mean as the sum over the two possible signs, divided by their number. The real values of the two signs are $-1$ and $1$, whose sum is zero; dividing by $2$ leaves zero.
