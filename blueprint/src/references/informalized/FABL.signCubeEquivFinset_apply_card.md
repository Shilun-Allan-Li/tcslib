# Positive-coordinate set cardinality

**Claim.** For every sign vector $x\in\{−1,1\}^k$, the cardinality of its positive-coordinate set is its positive-coordinate count.

**Proof.** By definition, the sign cube equivalence sends $x$ to its positive-coordinate set, and the positive-coordinate count is the cardinality of that same set. The two sides are therefore equal by unfolding the definitions.
