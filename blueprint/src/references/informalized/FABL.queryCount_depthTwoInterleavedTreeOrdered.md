The tree is built by running these initial queries in order, then applying the appropriate finishing procedure according to whether their observed signs agree. The disjoint-tree composition rule says that running one tree and then a continuation adds their query counts, with the continuation evaluated at the first tree's output; casting a tree to an equal coordinate set does not change its count. Substituting these identities into the two branchwise query totals gives exactly the two cases in the ordered model-cost formula; the remaining rearrangement is addition of natural numbers.
