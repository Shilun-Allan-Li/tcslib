# Base-Two Logarithm of Eight

**Claim.** The base-two logarithm of $8$ equals $3$.

**Proof.** Since $8=2^3$ and the base-two logarithm of $2$ is $1$, the logarithm power rule gives $\log_2 8=3\log_2 2=3$.
