# Evaluation of a dual Walsh character

**Claim.** The Walsh character associated with a linear functional $\psi$, evaluated at $x$, is the binary sign of $\psi(x)$.

**Proof.** By definition, the dual Walsh character is the binary sign composed with the additive homomorphism underlying $\psi$. Evaluating this composition at $x$ gives $\mathrm{binarySign}(\psi(x))$.
