# Composed Majority Influence Decomposition

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, the total influence of composed majority is its stated main term plus the influence error defined as the total influence minus that main term.

**Proof.** By definition, the influence error is the total influence of composed majority minus the main term. Adding the main term to this difference gives the total influence, by cancellation.
