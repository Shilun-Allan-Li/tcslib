# Nonnegative Arcsine Coefficients

**Claim.** For every natural number $j$, the arcsine series coefficient of index $j$ is nonnegative.

**Proof.** The coefficient is a quotient of the central binomial coefficient by positive powers of $2$ and by the positive integer $2j+1$. The binomial coefficient is nonnegative, and both denominators are positive, so the quotient is nonnegative.
