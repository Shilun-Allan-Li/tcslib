# Pointwise supremum norm bound

**Claim.** At every point of the binary cube, the absolute value of a real-valued function is at most its supremum norm over that cube.

**Proof.** The point $x$ belongs to the entire finite cube. The supremum of the values $|f(y)|$ over that cube is therefore at least the particular value $|f(x)|$, by the defining property of a finite supremum.
