# Fractional Power of Two

**Claim.** The real number $2^{3/2}$ is at most $3$.

**Proof.** Let $u=2^{3/2}$. Since $u\ge0$ and exponent multiplication gives $u^2=2^3=8$, comparison of squares reduces the claim to $8\le9$.
