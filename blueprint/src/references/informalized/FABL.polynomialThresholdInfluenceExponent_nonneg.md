# Nonnegative influence exponent

**Claim.** For every natural number $k$, the polynomial threshold influence exponent is nonnegative.

**Proof.** Since $2\ge 1$, its $k$th power is at least $1$. Taking reciprocals of these positive quantities shows $(2^k)^{-1}\le 1$. Therefore $1-(2^k)^{-1}\ge 0$, which is the claimed nonnegativity.
