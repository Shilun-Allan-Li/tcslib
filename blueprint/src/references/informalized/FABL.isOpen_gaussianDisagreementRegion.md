# Openness of the Gaussian disagreement region

**Claim.** In the correlation plane, the set of points whose two coordinates have product less than zero is open.

**Proof.** The coordinate projections on the correlation plane are continuous, so their product is a continuous real-valued function. The strict sublevel set where this product is less than zero is open, and it is exactly the Gaussian disagreement region.
