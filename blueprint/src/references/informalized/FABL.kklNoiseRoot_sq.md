# Square of the noise root

**Claim.** The square of the nonnegative square root of $1/3$ is $1/3$.

**Proof.** By the square-root identity, $(\sqrt{a})^2=a$ for every nonnegative real number $a$. Since $1/3\ge 0$, applying this identity with $a=1/3$ gives the result.
