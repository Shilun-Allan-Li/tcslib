# Vanishing reciprocal square root of the logarithm

**Claim.** The sequence $1/\sqrt{\log n}$ tends to zero as $n$ tends to infinity, with the reciprocal and square root taken in $\mathbb R$.

**Proof.** The natural numbers, viewed as real numbers, tend to $+\infty$, so their logarithms also tend to $+\infty$. Taking square roots preserves divergence to $+\infty$; taking reciprocals then gives convergence to zero.
