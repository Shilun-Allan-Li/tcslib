# Sign Encoding of Zero

**Claim.** The sign encoding of $0\in\mathbb{F}_2$ is $1$.

**Proof.** By definition, the sign encoding of $b$ is $(-1)^b$. At $b=0$, the zeroth power of $-1$ is the multiplicative identity, so the sign encoding is $1$.
