# Cardinality of the last two thirds

**Claim.** For a positive integer $m$, the integers in $\{0,\ldots,3m-1\}$ that are at least $m$ form a set of cardinality $2m$.

**Proof.** The set is the interval from $m$ up to, but not including, $3m$. Its cardinality is the endpoint difference, $3m-m=2m$.
