# Majority on Non-Tied Inputs

**Claim.** For every natural number $n$, majority agrees with the threshold sign of the coordinate sum on every sign vector whose coordinate sum is nonzero.

**Proof.** By definition, majority at any input is the threshold sign of that input's coordinate sum. Thus the asserted equality holds directly; the nonzero-sum hypothesis is not needed for this definitional identity.
