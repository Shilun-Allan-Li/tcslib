# Reciprocal Bound

**Claim.** For every natural number $D$ with $1\le D$, the rational number $1/D$ is positive and at most $1$.

**Proof.** The hypothesis gives $D\ge1$, also when viewed as a rational number. Since both numerator and denominator are positive, $1/D>0$. And because $D\ge1$, division of $1$ by $D$ gives a value at most $1$.
