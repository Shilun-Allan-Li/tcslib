# Dimension of a Binary Field Extension

**Claim.** If the modulus of a certified binary field implementation is an irreducible monic polynomial of degree $\ell$, then its quotient field has dimension $\ell$ over $\mathbb F_2$.

**Proof.** The adjunction of a root of an irreducible polynomial identifies the extension with the quotient of $\mathbb F_2[X]$ by the ideal generated by that polynomial. The standard quotient-dimension theorem for polynomials says that this quotient has vector-space dimension equal to the polynomial's natural degree. The modulus-degree identity (FABL.binaryMonicPolynomial_natDegree) gives degree $\ell$, so the extension has dimension $\ell$.
