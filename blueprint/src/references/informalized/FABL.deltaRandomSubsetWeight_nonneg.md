# Nonnegative Random Subset Weight

**Claim.** For $0\le\delta\le1$, the weight $\delta^{|J|}(1-\delta)^{n-|J|}$ is nonnegative.

**Proof.** The hypotheses give $\delta\ge0$ and $1-\delta\ge0$. Nonnegative real numbers have nonnegative natural powers, so both factors in the displayed weight are nonnegative. Their product is therefore nonnegative.
