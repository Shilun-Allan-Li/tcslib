# Coordinate vector at its index

**Claim.** The $i$th coordinate of the $i$th standard coordinate vector in $\mathbb{F}_2^n$ is $1$.

**Proof.** By definition, the $j$th coordinate of the standard vector indexed by $i$ is $1$ when $j=i$ and $0$ otherwise. Taking $j=i$ gives $1$.
