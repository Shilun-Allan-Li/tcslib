# Agreement of finite-cube monomials

**Claim.** For a finite set of coordinates and a sign assignment on the finite cube, the indexed monomial equals the finite-cube monomial.

**Proof.** Both monomials are the same finite product: at each coordinate in the set, they use the sign value of the assignment at that coordinate. Their definitions therefore agree directly.
