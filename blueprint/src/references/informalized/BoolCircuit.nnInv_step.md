# Preservation of the circuit invariant under one step

Its new tap map assigns the base index to the newly computed gate; for each old argument it assigns a distinct later index, and otherwise it retains the old tap. The strictness and acyclicity assumptions ensure that all arguments of $g$ were valid old vertices and their taps lie below the base. The proof checks the invariant components in turn. The tap bounds and injectivity follow by separating the new gate's index, the old arguments, and untouched old vertices; the old tap bounds and injectivity handle the untouched vertices, while distinct old arguments have distinct taps. For values, old vertices keep their values when gates are appended (`vertexValue_append`). At the new gate's index, the remapped copy computes the same operation on the tapped inputs as the original gate, by the remapping evaluation lemma (`DAGGate.eval_remap`) and the old value invariant. For an argument of $g$, the two NOT gates restore its old tapped value, and untouched vertices retain their old values. The new tapped vertices are likewise unused by the new gates, so the fanout bounds and tap-unused condition follow.
