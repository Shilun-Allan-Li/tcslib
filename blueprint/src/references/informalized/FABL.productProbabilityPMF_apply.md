# Mass of the product probability

**Claim.** For each $x\in\Omega^n$, the product distribution with independent coordinate law $\pi$ assigns mass $\prod_{i=1}^n\pi(x_i)$.

**Proof.** Unfolding the product distribution shows that its mass at $x$ is defined to be the product of the coordinate masses, namely $\prod_i\pi(x_i)$.
