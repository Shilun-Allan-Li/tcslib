# First parity coordinate

**Claim.** For any pair of signs $(a,b)$, the first coordinate of the associated parity triple is $a$.

**Proof.** The parity triple is defined by the three-entry vector $(a,b,-ab)$. Its first coordinate is therefore $a$ by the definition of the vector.
