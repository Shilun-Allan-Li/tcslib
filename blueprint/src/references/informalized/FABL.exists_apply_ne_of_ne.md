# A differing coordinate

**Claim.** If two assignments of signs to $r$ coordinates are distinct, then they differ at some coordinate.

**Proof.** If there were no coordinate at which the assignments differ, they would agree at every coordinate. Extensionality of functions would then make the assignments equal, contrary to the hypothesis. Therefore some coordinate has different assigned signs.
