# Continuity of Euclidean coordinates

**Claim.** The map sending a point of the real plane with the $2$-norm to the Euclidean plane point with the same two real coordinates is continuous.

**Proof.** Passing from the $2$-norm plane to its underlying pair of real coordinates is continuous, as is the map that sends a pair to the Euclidean-space point with those coordinates. Their composition is the stated coordinate map, so it is continuous.
