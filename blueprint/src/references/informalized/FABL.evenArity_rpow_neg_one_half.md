# Negative half power

**Claim.** For every nonnegative integer $m$, $(2m+2)^{-1/2}=1/\sqrt{2m+2}$.

**Proof.** The number $N=2m+2$ is positive. For positive bases, a negative real power is the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power, so $N^{-1/2}=1/N^{1/2}$. The square-root identity $N^{1/2}=\sqrt N$ gives the result.
