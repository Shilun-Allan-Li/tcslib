# Negative Three Halves Power Identity

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, $(2m+1)^{-3/2}=1\big/((2m+1)\sqrt{2m+1})$.

**Proof.** The base $2m+1$ is positive. Rewrite the exponent as $-(1+1/2)$. The power-of-a-sum rule expresses the result as the reciprocal of $(2m+1)^1(2m+1)^{1/2}$. These factors are $2m+1$ and its square root, respectively, giving the identity.
