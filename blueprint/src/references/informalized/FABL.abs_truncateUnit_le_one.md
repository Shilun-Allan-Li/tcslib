# Bound on the Truncated Value

**Claim.** For every real $z$, its projection onto $[-1,1]$ has absolute value at most $1$.

**Proof.** The projection of $z$ onto $[-1,1]$ belongs to that interval. Hence its value lies between $-1$ and $1$, which is exactly the condition that its absolute value is at most $1$.
