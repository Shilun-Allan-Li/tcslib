# Balanced Exponent Ceiling Bound

**Claim.** For a positive rational matrix exponent $\omega$ and a natural number $k$, the balanced exponent is at most its ceiling, the integer cutoff.

**Proof.** By definition, the cutoff is the natural-number ceiling of the balanced exponent. The ceiling is at least the number whose ceiling is taken, so the balanced exponent is at most the cutoff viewed as a rational.
