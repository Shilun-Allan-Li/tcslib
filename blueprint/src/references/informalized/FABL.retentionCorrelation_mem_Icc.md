# Range of the retention correlation

**Claim.** If $\rho\in[0,1]$, then its retention correlation $2\rho-1$ lies in $[-1,1]$.

**Proof.** The lower bound $\rho\ge0$ gives $2\rho-1\ge-1$, while $\rho\le1$ gives $2\rho-1\le1$. Together these are exactly membership in the interval $[-1,1]$.
