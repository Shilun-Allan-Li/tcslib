# Symmetry of the standard Gaussian density

**Claim.** For every real $t$, the standard Gaussian density at $-t$ equals its value at $t$.

**Proof.** In the standard Gaussian density formula, replacing $t$ by $-t$ leaves the exponent unchanged because $(-t)^2=t^2$. The normalizing factor is also unchanged, so the two density values are equal.
