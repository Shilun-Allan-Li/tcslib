# Nonnegative Switching Atom Ratio

**Claim.** For $0\le\delta<1$, the switching atom ratio $\frac{2\delta}{1-\delta}$ is nonnegative.

**Proof.** By hypothesis, $2\delta\ge 0$. Also $1-\delta>0$, so dividing the nonnegative numerator by this positive denominator preserves nonnegativity.
