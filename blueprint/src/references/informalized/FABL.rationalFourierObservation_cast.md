# Rational Fourier observation cast

**Claim.** The rational observation formed from a sign label and the coordinate monomial, regarded as a real number, equals the real Fourier observation.

**Proof.** Substitute the sample label $f(x)$ into the rational observation. Casting its label and each coordinate sign from integers to reals preserves multiplication and finite products. The resulting expression is the real label $f(x)$ times the product of the signs on $S$, which is precisely the real Fourier observation by its definition.
