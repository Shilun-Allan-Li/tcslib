# Positive Bribery Rate

**Claim.** For every integer $n\ge2$, the quantity $\frac{\log n}{100n}$ is positive, with logarithm and arithmetic interpreted over the reals.

**Proof.** The hypothesis gives $n>1$, so $\log n>0$. Also $100n>0$. Dividing a positive numerator by a positive denominator proves that the bribery rate is positive.
