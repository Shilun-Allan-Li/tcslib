# Free Component of the Sign Cube Split

**Claim.** For a sign assignment on all coordinates, its restriction to $J$ is the first component of its decomposition into assignments on $J$ and on the complement.

**Proof.** Two assignments on $J$ are equal if they agree at every coordinate in $J. At any such coordinate, the first component of the split equivalence evaluates the original assignment at that same coordinate. It therefore agrees pointwise with the restriction of the original assignment, proving equality.
