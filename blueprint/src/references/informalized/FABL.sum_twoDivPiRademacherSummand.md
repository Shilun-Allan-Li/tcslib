# Signed Linear-Form Summands

**Claim.** The sum of the coordinate summands, each coefficient multiplied by the corresponding sign coordinate, is the linear form defined by those coefficients and coordinates.

**Proof.** By definition, the Rademacher summand at coordinate $i$ is $a_i$ times the sign value of the $i$th coordinate. The linear form is defined as the sum of exactly these same terms over all coordinates. The two expressions therefore agree term by term.
