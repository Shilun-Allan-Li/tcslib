# Zero in the right argument

**Claim.** For any vector $s\in(\mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z})^n$, the dot product of $s$ with the zero vector is zero.

**Proof.** Every summand in the dot product is $s_i\cdot 0=0$. Therefore their finite sum is zero.
