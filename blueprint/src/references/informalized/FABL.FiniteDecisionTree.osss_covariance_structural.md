# Structural OSSS Covariance Bound

**Claim.** For sign-valued functions $f$ and $g$ on a finite product space, if a decision tree computes $f$, then their covariance is at most the sum over coordinates of the tree's structural query probability times the influence of $g$.

**Proof.** Induct on the tree. At a leaf, $f$ is constant. Its covariance with $g$ is zero (`productCovariance_const_left`), and the right side is nonnegative because every query probability is zero and each influence is nonnegative (`productInfluence_nonneg`). Now suppose the root queries coordinate $j$. For each outcome $\omega$, the child tree computes $f$ with coordinate $j$ fixed to $\omega$: replacing that coordinate in an input does not change the child's evaluation, since it is unavailable to the child (`eval_eq_of_agreeOn`). The sign-range hypotheses persist under this coordinate fixing (`hasSignRange_fixProductCoordinate`), so induction applies to each child and its restricted functions. Averaging these child bounds over independent outcomes yields a bound on the restriction covariance by the sum of the child query probabilities times the corresponding influences. For coordinates other than $j$, the average influence of the restricted version of $g$ is its original influence (`pmfExpectation_productInfluence_fixProductCoordinate`); the child query probability at $j$ is zero because $j$ is no longer available (`structuralQueryProbability_eq_zero_of_not_mem`). The covariance decomposition writes the original covariance as this restriction covariance plus the inner product of the two coordinate Laplacians (`productCovariance_eq_restrictionAverage_add_laplacian`). The Laplacian inner product is at most the influence of $g$ at $j$ (`FiniteFourierBasis.productInner_laplacian_laplacian_le_influence`). Finally, the root query probability is $1$ at $j$ and is the average child query probability at every other coordinate, so these terms combine to give exactly the stated sum.
