# Odd Frequencies in Dimension Two

**Claim.** The odd-cardinality subsets of $\{0,1\}$ are exactly $\{0\}$ and $\{1\}$.

**Proof.** The subsets of $\{0,1\}$ are the empty set, the two singleton sets, and the full set. Their cardinalities are respectively $0,1,1,2$, so precisely the two singleton sets have odd cardinality.
