# Third coordinate of a three-sign vector

**Claim.** The vector formed from signs $(a,b,c)$ has value $c$ at coordinate $2$.

**Proof.** At coordinate $2$, neither the case for coordinate $0$ nor the case for coordinate $1$ applies. The final case in the definition therefore gives $c$.
