# Positivity of Majority Influence

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, $\binom{2m}{m}/2^{2m}$ is positive.

**Proof.** The central binomial coefficient $\binom{2m}{m}$ is positive because $m\le 2m$. The denominator $2^{2m}$ is also positive. Their quotient is therefore positive.
