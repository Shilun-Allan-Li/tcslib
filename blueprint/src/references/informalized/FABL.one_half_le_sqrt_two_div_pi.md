# Lower bound for the Gaussian constant

**Claim.** The real number $\sqrt{2/\pi}$ is at least $1/2$.

**Proof.** Because $0<\pi<4$, we have $1/4\le2/\pi$: multiplying by the positive number $\pi$ reduces this to $\pi\le8$, which follows from $\pi<4$. Both sides of $\sqrt{2/\pi}\ge1/2$ are nonnegative, so the squared inequality implies the desired bound.
