# Keep probability at one

**Claim.** Given that $1\in[-1,1]$, the keep probability at correlation $\rho=1$, viewed as an extended nonnegative real number, is one.

**Proof.** By definition, the keep probability at $\rho=1$ is the nonnegative real number represented by $(1+1)/2$, which equals one. Coercing this value into the extended nonnegative reals gives one there as well.
