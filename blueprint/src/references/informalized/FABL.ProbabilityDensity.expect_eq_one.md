# Mean-One Property of a Probability Density

**Claim.** Every probability density $\varphi$ on $\mathbb F_2^n$ has uniform expectation $1$.

**Proof.** This is exactly the mean-one condition in the definition of a probability density: its uniform expectation is $1$.
