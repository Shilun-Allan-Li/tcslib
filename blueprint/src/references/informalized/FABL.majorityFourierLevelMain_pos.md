# Positivity of the Main Fourier Term

**Claim.** For every positive integer $k$, the main Fourier term $(2/\pi)^{3/2}k^{-3/2}$ is positive.

**Proof.** Both $2/\pi$ and $k$ are positive. Real powers of a positive number are positive, so each factor in $(2/\pi)^{3/2}k^{-3/2}$ is positive, and hence their product is positive.
