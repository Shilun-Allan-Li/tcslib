# Singleton Mass Under the Uniform Pair Law

**Claim.** Every singleton bit-pair has mass $1/4$ under the uniform probability law on the four ordered Boolean pairs.

**Proof.** By definition, the pair law is uniform on the full set of Boolean pairs. The uniform-measure formula assigns a subset real measure equal to its cardinality divided by the cardinality of the full space. A singleton has cardinality one, while the pair space has cardinality four, so its measure is $1/4$.
