# Positivity of Biased Sign Mass

**Claim.** For every real number $p$ with $0<p<1$ and every sign $b\in\{\pm1\}$, the biased sign mass at $b$ is positive.

**Proof.** Every sign is either $1$ or $-1$. If $b=1$, its mass is $1-p$, which is positive because $p<1$. If $b=-1$, its mass is $p$, which is positive because $p>0$.
