# Square of the Noise Root

**Claim.** The square of the nonnegative square root of $1/5$ equals $1/5$.

**Proof.** By definition, the noise root is $\sqrt{1/5}$. Since $1/5\ge 0$, the square of its real square root is the original number; therefore the square is $1/5$.
