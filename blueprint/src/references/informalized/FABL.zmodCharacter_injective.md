# Injectivity of cyclic characters

**Claim.** For every positive modulus, the map sending a residue class to its cyclic character is injective.

**Proof.** The cyclic-character map is the additive equivalence from residues modulo $m$ to additive characters, evaluated at its input. Since an equivalence is injective, two residues that give the same character must be equal.
