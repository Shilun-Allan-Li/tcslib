# Reciprocal Parameter Identity

**Claim.** For every real number $\eta>0$, $2/\eta+1$ equals the reciprocal of $\eta/(2+\eta)$.

**Proof.** Because $\eta>0$, both $\eta$ and $2+\eta$ are nonzero. Thus the reciprocal of $\eta/(2+\eta)$ is $(2+\eta)/\eta$. Splitting this quotient gives $2/\eta+1$, as required.
