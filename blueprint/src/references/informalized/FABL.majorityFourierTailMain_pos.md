# Positivity of the Main Tail Term

**Claim.** For every positive integer $k$, the main Fourier tail term $(2/\pi)^{3/2}k^{-1/2}$ is positive.

**Proof.** The base $2/\pi$ and the integer $k$ are positive. Their real powers with exponents $3/2$ and $-1/2$ are therefore positive, so their product is positive.
