# The only elements of the binary field

**Claim.** If $x\in\mathbb F_2$ and $x\ne1$, then $x=0$.

**Proof.** Represent $x$ by its least nonnegative residue modulo $2$, which is either $0$ or $1$. Since $x\ne1$, its residue cannot be $1$; hence it is $0$, and therefore $x=0$.
