# Real-Valued Granularity Formula

**Claim.** For every natural number $k$, the real value of the degree-$k$ Fourier granularity is $2(1/2)^k$.

**Proof.** The granularity is defined over the rationals as $2(2^{-1})^k$. Casting this expression to the reals preserves multiplication, powers, and the rational inverse of $2$, giving $2(2^{-1})^k$ in $\mathbb R$.
