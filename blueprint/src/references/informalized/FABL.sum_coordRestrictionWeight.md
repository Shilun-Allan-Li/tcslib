# Total weight of one-coordinate restrictions

**Claim.** The weights of the free, fixed-to-$1$, and fixed-to-$-1$ restrictions, respectively $1/2$, $1/4$, and $1/4$, sum to $1$.

**Proof.** There are exactly three restrictions. Adding their assigned weights gives $1/2+1/4+1/4=1$.
