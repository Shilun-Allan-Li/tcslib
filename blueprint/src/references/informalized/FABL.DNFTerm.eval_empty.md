# Evaluation of the empty term

**Claim.** For every dimension and every sign vector, the empty conjunction evaluates to $-1$.

**Proof.** The empty term has no literals, so the condition that all its literals evaluate to $-1$ holds vacuously. By the definition of term evaluation, the empty conjunction therefore evaluates to $-1$.
