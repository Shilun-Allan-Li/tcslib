# Combined sign vector on free coordinates

**Claim.** At every index in $J$, the sign vector formed by combining assignments on $J$ and its complement agrees with the assignment on $J$.

**Proof.** The combined vector is defined by applying the inverse of the coordinate-splitting equivalence to the pair of assignments. At an index in $J$, the inverse splitting equivalence recovers the component indexed by $J$. Hence its value is the prescribed sign there.
