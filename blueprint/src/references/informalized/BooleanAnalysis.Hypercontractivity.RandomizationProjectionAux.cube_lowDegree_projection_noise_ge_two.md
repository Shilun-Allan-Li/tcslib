# Higher moment bound for a low degree projection

**Claim.** For $q\ge2$ and $0<\rho\le1$, the $q$-moment norm of the degree-$k$ truncation of $g$ is at most $(\sqrt{q-1}/\rho)^k$ times the $q$-moment norm of the noise operator applied to $g$.

**Proof.** Set $r=1/\rho$ and let $f$ be the degree-$k$ truncation of the noise-smoothed function. Then $r\ge1$. The coefficient formulas for noise and truncation (`BooleanAnalysis.noiseOp_fourier` and `KKL.fourierCoeff_lowDegreePart`) show that applying noise with parameter $r$ to $f$ recovers the degree-$k$ truncation of $g$: on sets of size at most $k$, the multipliers combine to $(r\rho)^{|S|}=1$, and on larger sets the coefficient is zero. The degree-$k$ moment estimate (`BooleanAnalysis.Hypercontractivity.lowDegree_norm_ge_two`), applied using that the truncation has degree at most $k$ (`BooleanAnalysis.Hypercontractivity.RandomizationProjectionAux.cube_lowDegree_has_degree`), bounds its $q$-moment norm by $\sqrt{q-1}^{\,k}$ times its $L^2$ norm. The noise operator estimate (`BooleanAnalysis.Hypercontractivity.l2Norm_noiseOp_le`) bounds the latter, after the recovery identity, by $r^k$ times the $L^2$ norm of $f$. The truncation contracts the $L^2$ norm (`BooleanAnalysis.Hypercontractivity.RandomizationProjectionAux.cube_lowDegree_l2_le`), so this is at most $r^k$ times the $L^2$ norm of the smoothed function. Finally, the $L^2$ norm equals its $2$-moment norm (`BooleanAnalysis.Hypercontractivity.cubeLpNorm_two`), and monotonicity of moment norms on a probability space (`BooleanAnalysis.Hypercontractivity.lp_norm_mono`) bounds that by its $q$-moment norm. Combining the factors gives $\sqrt{q-1}^{\,k}r^k=(\sqrt{q-1}/\rho)^k$.
