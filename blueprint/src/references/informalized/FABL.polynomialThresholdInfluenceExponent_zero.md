# Zero Influence Exponent at Zero

**Claim.** The exponent $1-2^{-k}$ equals $0$ when $k=0$.

**Proof.** At $k=0$, the defining expression is $1-((2:\mathbb{R})^0)^{-1}$. Since $2^0=1$ and $1^{-1}=1$, this is $1-1=0$.
