# Positivity of the Real Cardinality

**Claim.** If a finite set has at least two elements, its cardinality regarded as a real number is positive.

**Proof.** Its natural-number cardinality is at least two, and therefore is strictly positive. Casting this inequality to the real numbers preserves strict order, so the real cardinality is positive.
