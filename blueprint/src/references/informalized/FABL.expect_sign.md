# Mean of a uniform random sign

**Claim.** For every real-valued function on the two signs, its uniform mean is the average of its values at $1$ and $-1$.

**Proof.** The uniform expectation on the two-element sign set is the sum of the function's values divided by the set's cardinality. The two signs are exactly $1$ and $-1$, so this gives $(g(1)+g(-1))/2$.
