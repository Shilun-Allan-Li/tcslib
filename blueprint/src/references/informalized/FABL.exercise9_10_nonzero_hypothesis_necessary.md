# Failure at a zero numerator

**Claim.** The inequality $1/2\le\sqrt{(1-1)/(2-1)}$ is false.

**Proof.** The numerator is zero and the denominator is one, so the square-root side is $0$. The proposed inequality would say $1/2\le0$, which is false.
