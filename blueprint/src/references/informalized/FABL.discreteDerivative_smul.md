# Homogeneity of the Discrete Derivative

**Claim.** For a real scalar $c$, the discrete derivative in coordinate $i$ of $cf$ is $c$ times the discrete derivative of $f$.

**Proof.** The discrete derivative in coordinate $i$ is a real-linear map on functions. Its compatibility with scalar multiplication therefore gives the derivative of $c f$ as $c$ times the derivative of $f$, as asserted.
