# Sum of Rademacher Summands

**Claim.** The sum of the Rademacher summands $a_i x_i$ is the linear form $\sum_{i=1}^n a_i x_i$.

**Proof.** For each index $i$, the Rademacher summand is defined to be $a_i$ times the sign value of $x_i$, and the linear form is defined as the sum of precisely these terms over all indices. Thus the two expressions agree term by term by unfolding their definitions.
