# Free coordinates of a restriction

**Claim.** Given a coordinate set $J$ and signs assigned outside $J$, the coordinate restriction determined by these data is free at every coordinate in $J$.

**Proof.** At a coordinate $i\in J$, the definition selects the free restriction in its first case. The assigned sign is used only in the alternative case, so the restriction at $i$ is free.
