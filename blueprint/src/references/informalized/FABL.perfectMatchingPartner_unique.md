# Uniqueness of the matching partner

**Claim.** If $v$ and $w$ are joined by an edge in a perfect matching, then $w$ is the partner of $v$.

**Proof.** For each vertex in a perfect matching, the matching property specifies a unique adjacent vertex. Since $w$ is adjacent to $v$, uniqueness identifies $w$ with the chosen partner of $v$.
