# Viola Error at Zero

**Claim.** For every natural number $d$, the Viola error $V(0,d)$ is zero.

**Proof.** The exponent $1/2^{d-1}$ is nonzero, since its denominator is positive. A real power of zero with nonzero exponent is zero, so the defining expression gives $V(0,d)=9\cdot0=0$.
