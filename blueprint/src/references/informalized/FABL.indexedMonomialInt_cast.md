# Integer and real indexed monomials

**Claim.** The integer product of the signs over a finite set, regarded as a real number, equals the real indexed monomial over that set.

**Proof.** Casting a finite product of integers into the reals gives the product of their real casts. For each index, the real cast of the integer underlying its sign is exactly the sign value used in the real monomial. The two products therefore agree.
