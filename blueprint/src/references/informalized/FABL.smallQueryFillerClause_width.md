# Width of the filler clause

**Claim.** For $r\in\mathbb N$ and positive $K$, the filler clause on $r+K$ coordinates has width exactly $1$.

**Proof.** The filler clause is constructed from a single literal. Its width is the number of literals in its list, which is the length of a singleton list and therefore equals $1$.
