# Bilinear form and dot product

**Claim.** For every $n$ and $x,y\in\mathbb{F}_2^n$, the bilinear form defined by the coordinate dot product, evaluated at $x$ and $y$, equals their dot product.

**Proof.** By definition, the bilinear form is the linear map associated to the dot-product pairing. Evaluating that map at $x$ and then at $y$ therefore gives exactly the coordinate dot product of $x$ and $y$.
