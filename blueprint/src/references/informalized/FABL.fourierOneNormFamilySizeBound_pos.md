# Positivity of the family-size bound

**Claim.** For any real $s$ and any allowed parameter $\varepsilon$, the integer $\max\{1,\lceil4s^2/\varepsilon\rceil\}$ is positive.

**Proof.** The maximum is at least its first entry, $1$. Thus it is a positive natural number.
