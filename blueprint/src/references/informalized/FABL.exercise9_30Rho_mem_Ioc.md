# Range of the ratio parameter

**Claim.** If $0<\delta<1$, then $(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)$ is positive and at most $1$.

**Proof.** The numerator $1-\delta$ is positive and the denominator $1+\delta$ is positive, so their quotient is positive. Since $\delta>0$, we also have $1-\delta\le1+\delta$; division by the positive denominator gives $(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)\le1$.
