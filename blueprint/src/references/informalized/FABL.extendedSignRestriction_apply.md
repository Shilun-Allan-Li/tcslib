# Evaluation of an Extended Restriction

**Claim.** The extended sign restriction of $f$, evaluated at $x$, is $f$ evaluated at the vector that uses $x$ on $J$ and the fixed signs $z$ outside $J$.

**Proof.** By definition, the extended restriction sends $x$ to $f$ applied to the combination of the restriction of $x$ to the free coordinates $J$ and the fixed assignment $z$. This is exactly the displayed equality.
