# Affine sign function evaluation

**Claim.** The affine sign function at $x$ is the product of the real value of $\sigma$ and the Walsh character indexed by $\gamma$ evaluated at $x$.

**Proof.** Unfold the affine sign function as its modulation applied to the constant function with value $1$. The modulation multiplies the sign value, the Walsh character, and that constant; removing the factor $1$ gives the asserted identity.
