# Nonempty Selected Fourier Family

**Claim.** The family of subsets of $J$ of size at most the prescribed degree is nonempty.

**Proof.** The empty set is a subset of $J$ of size zero, and zero is among the degrees included in the low-degree family. Hence the empty set belongs to the selected Fourier family, proving nonemptiness.
