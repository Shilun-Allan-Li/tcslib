# Exponent identity for the ratio parameter

**Claim.** For $0<\delta<1$, if $\rho=(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)$, then $2/(1+\rho)=1+\delta$.

**Proof.** Substitute the definition of $\rho$ and combine the terms in the denominator: $1+\rho=1+(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)=2/(1+\delta)$. Since $1+\delta\ne0$, taking the reciprocal and multiplying by $2$ gives the stated identity.
