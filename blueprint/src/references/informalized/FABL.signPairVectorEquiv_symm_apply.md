# Inverse Pair Coordinates

**Claim.** The inverse of the coordinatewise pairing equivalence sends each pair of sign-valued vectors to the vector whose coordinate $i$ is the pair of their $i$th entries.

**Proof.** By the definition of the equivalence, its inverse sends a pair of vectors to the function that at each coordinate returns the pair of their entries. Thus evaluation at $i$ gives $(x_i,y_i)$ directly.
