# Symmetry of the binary dot product

**Claim.** For every $n$ and all $x,y\in\mathbb{F}_2^n$, the coordinate dot product of $x$ and $y$ equals that of $y$ and $x$.

**Proof.** The bilinear form is given by the coordinate dot product. Its values at $(x,y)$ and $(y,x)$ are equal by commutativity of the dot product, so the form is symmetric.
