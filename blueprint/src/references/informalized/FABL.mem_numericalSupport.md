# Numerical support membership

**Claim.** A set $S$ belongs to the numerical support of $c$ exactly when $c(S)\ne 0$.

**Proof.** The numerical support is defined as the family of sets in the universal family for which the coefficient is nonzero. Since $S$ is one of the sets in that universal family, membership is equivalent to $c(S)\ne0$.
