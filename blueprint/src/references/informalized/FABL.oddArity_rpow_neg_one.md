# Reciprocal Power of an Odd Integer

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, the real power $(2m+1)^{-1}$ equals $1/(2m+1)$.

**Proof.** The base $2m+1$ is positive. The negative-one power of a positive real is its reciprocal, so $(2m+1)^{-1}=1/(2m+1)$.
