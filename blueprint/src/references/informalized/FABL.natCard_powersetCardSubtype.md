# Cardinality of Fixed-Size Subsets

**Claim.** For a finite set $R$ and a natural number $d$, the number of its $d$-element subsets is $\binom{|R|}{d}$.

**Proof.** The subtype of the $d$-element subsets of $R$ is finite, and its cardinality is its natural-number cardinality. The standard finite-set counting identity for the $d$-element subsets of a finite set says that this cardinality is $|R|$ choose $d$.
