# Elements of the binary field

**Claim.** Every element $a$ of the field with two elements is either $0$ or $1$.

**Proof.** There are exactly two elements in $\mathbb F_2$, represented by the residues $0$ and $1$. Thus $a$ equals one of these, giving the stated alternatives.
