# Mean of a Uniform Sign

**Claim.** A uniform sign has mean zero: the average of its real value over the two signs $-1$ and $1$ is $0$.

**Proof.** Expand the uniform mean as the sum of the real sign values divided by the number of signs. The two values are $-1$ and $1$, so their sum is zero and hence their average is zero.
