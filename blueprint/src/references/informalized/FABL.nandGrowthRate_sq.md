# NAND growth rate identity

**Claim.** For $r=(1+\sqrt{33})/4$, one has $r^2=r/2+2$.

**Proof.** Put $s=\sqrt{33}$, so $s^2=33$, and $r=(1+s)/4$. Then $r^2=(1+2s+s^2)/16=(34+2s)/16=(17+s)/8$. Also $r/2+2=(1+s)/8+2=(17+s)/8$. These expressions are equal.
