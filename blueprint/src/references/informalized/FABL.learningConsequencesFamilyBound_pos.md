# Positivity of the family bound

**Claim.** For every positive influence bound $t$ and positive learning parameter $\varepsilon$, the family bound $(d+1)(k+1)^d$ is positive.

**Proof.** Unfold the family bound. Its first factor is the successor of the natural number $d$, hence is positive. Its second factor is a natural power of the positive successor $k+1$, so it too is positive. The product of these positive factors is positive.
