# Combined assignment on fixed coordinates

**Claim.** The assignment obtained by combining a sign assignment on $P$ with one on the complementary coordinates agrees with the assignment on $P$ at every coordinate in $P$.

**Proof.** At a coordinate in $P$, the defining case distinction for the combined assignment selects the fixed-coordinate assignment. Evaluating there therefore gives exactly its value at that coordinate.
