# Total Variation Mass and Jordan Parts

**Claim.** For a signed measure s, the total mass of its total variation measure equals the sum of the total masses of its positive and negative Jordan parts.

**Proof.** The total variation measure is the sum of the positive and negative parts in the Jordan decomposition. Evaluating this measure identity on the whole space and using additivity of the real-valued measure gives the stated equality.
