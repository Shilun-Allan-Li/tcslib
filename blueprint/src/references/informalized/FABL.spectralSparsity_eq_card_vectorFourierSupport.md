# Spectral Sparsity Counts Fourier Support

**Claim.** Spectral sparsity equals the cardinality of the support of the vector Fourier coefficients, that is, the number of frequencies with nonzero coefficient.

**Proof.** Both sides are defined as the cardinality of the same finite set: the frequencies whose vector Fourier coefficient is nonzero. Hence they are equal.
