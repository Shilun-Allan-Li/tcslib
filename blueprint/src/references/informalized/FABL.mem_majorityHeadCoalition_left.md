# Left-summand membership in the head coalition

**Claim.** The index corresponding to $(i,\mathrm{left})$ under the canonical identification of the disjoint union of sets of sizes $k$ and $m$ belongs to the coalition of the first $k$ indices.

**Proof.** The head coalition is the image of all indices in the $k$-element set under the embedding that places them in the first summand. The given index is exactly the image of $i$ under this embedding, so it belongs to that image.
