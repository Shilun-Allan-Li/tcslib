# Nonvanishing of the Enumerated Vectors

**Claim.** When $n\le 2^\ell-1$, every vector assigned to an index by the enumeration of nonzero vectors in $\mathbb F_2^\ell$ is nonzero.

**Proof.** The enumeration is obtained from an equivalence whose codomain consists of vectors equipped with a proof that they are nonzero. Evaluating the equivalence at the included index therefore supplies exactly the required nonvanishing property.
