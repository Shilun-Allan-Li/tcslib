# Keep probability at negative one

**Claim.** Given that $-1\in[-1,1]$, the keep probability at correlation $\rho=-1$, viewed as an extended nonnegative real number, is zero.

**Proof.** By definition, the keep probability at $\rho=-1$ is the nonnegative real number represented by $(1+(-1))/2$. This real number is zero. Its image in the extended nonnegative reals is therefore also zero.
