# Cyclic Fourier basis vectors

**Claim.** For every positive integer $m$ and residue $j$ modulo $m$, the Fourier basis vector indexed by $j$ is the cyclic character indexed by $j$.

**Proof.** The cyclic Fourier basis is obtained by reindexing the character basis along the inverse of the standard equivalence between residues and characters. Thus the basis vector at $j$ is the character corresponding to $j$, which is precisely the cyclic character indexed by $j.
