# Square of a threshold derivative summand

**Claim.** For a real-valued function $p$ on the sign cube, a coordinate $i$, and an input $x$, the square of the product of the $i$th input sign and the real-valued threshold of the $i$th discrete derivative of $p$ at $x$ is $1$.

**Proof.** Both the input sign $x_i$ and the thresholded derivative take values in $\{1,-1\}$. Their real-valued product is therefore either $1$ or $-1$, and either value has square $1$.
