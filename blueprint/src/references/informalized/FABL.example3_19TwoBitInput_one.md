# Second coordinate of the two-bit input

**Claim.** For any assignment of signs to coordinates $0$ and $1$, the constructed two-bit input has the assigned sign at coordinate $1$.

**Proof.** The two-bit input is formed as the ordered vector whose first entry is the sign assigned to coordinate $0$ and whose second entry is the sign assigned to coordinate $1$. Thus its coordinate $1$ is the latter sign.
