# Majority Tail Dimension Bound

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, $\sqrt{2m+1}\le\sqrt{2m+3}$.

**Proof.** The natural numbers satisfy $2m+1\le2m+3$. Both are nonnegative, and the square root is monotone on nonnegative reals; applying this monotonicity gives the stated inequality.
