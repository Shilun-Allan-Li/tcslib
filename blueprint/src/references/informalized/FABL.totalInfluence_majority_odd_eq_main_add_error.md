# Odd Majority Error Decomposition

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, the total influence of majority on $2m+1$ coordinates is its main term $\sqrt{2/\pi}\sqrt{2m+1}$ plus the odd-majority error.

**Proof.** By definition, the odd-majority error is the total influence in dimension $2m+1$ minus the stated main term. Rearranging this defining identity gives the claimed equality.
