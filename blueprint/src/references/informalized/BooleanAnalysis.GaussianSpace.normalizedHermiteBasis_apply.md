# Normalized Hermite basis action

**Claim.** For each nonnegative integer $k$, the $k$th element of the basis formed from normalized Hermite polynomials is the normalized Hermite polynomial of degree $k$.

**Proof.** By definition, the basis is obtained from the sequence whose $k$th polynomial is the normalized Hermite polynomial of degree $k$. The basis construction therefore assigns that very polynomial to the $k$th basis element.
