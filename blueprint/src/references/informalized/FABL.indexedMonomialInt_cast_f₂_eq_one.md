# Indexed sign product modulo two

**Claim.** The integer product of the signs over any finite set, reduced modulo two, equals $1$ in the field with two elements.

**Proof.** Cast the integer product into $\mathbb F_2$; this turns it into the product of the individual signs' casts. Each sign is either $1$ or $-1$ as an integer, and both reduce to $1$ modulo two. Hence every factor is $1$, so their finite product is $1.
