# Subset of an Intersection

**Claim.** If $U\cap J=S$, then $S\subseteq U$.

**Proof.** Take any $x\in S$. The equality $U\cap J=S$ implies $x\in U\cap J$, so $x\in U$ by the definition of intersection. Hence $S\subseteq U$.
