# Sparsity Bound Lower Estimate

**Claim.** For every natural number $k$, the quantity $2^{k+1}$ is at least $2$.

**Proof.** The power $2^k$ is positive. Using $2^{k+1}=2^k\cdot2$, positivity gives $2^k\ge1$ because it is an integer. Multiplying by $2$ yields $2^{k+1}\ge2$.
