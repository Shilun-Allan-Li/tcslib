# Pairing Joined Binary Blocks

**Claim.** The dot product of the concatenations of a,b and x,y is the sum of the dot products of a with x and b with y.

**Proof.** Expand the dot product over the concatenated coordinates. The first block contributes the sum of aᵢxᵢ, and the second contributes the sum of bᵢyᵢ. Combining these two sums gives the sum of the two blockwise dot products.
