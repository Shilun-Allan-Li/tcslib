# Three-halves power identity

**Claim.** For every positive real number $a$, $a^{3/2}=a\sqrt a$.

**Proof.** Use the identity $\sqrt a=a^{1/2}$ for positive $a$. Since $3/2=1+1/2$, the addition law for real powers gives $a^{3/2}=a^1a^{1/2}$; the first factor is $a$. Substituting the square-root identity proves the result.
