# Existence of an Odd Fourier Frequency

**Claim.** For every positive natural number $n$, there is an odd-cardinality subset of $\{0,1,\ldots,n-1\}$.

**Proof.** Since $n>0$, the index set contains $0$. Take the singleton consisting of that index. Its cardinality is one, which is odd, so it belongs to the family of odd-cardinality subsets and witnesses that the family is nonempty.
