# A Bound Involving Pi

**Claim.** The square root of $2/\pi$ is at most $1$.

**Proof.** Since $\pi>3$, we have $2/\pi<1$. Both sides are nonnegative, and monotonicity of the square root therefore gives $\sqrt{2/\pi}\le1$.
