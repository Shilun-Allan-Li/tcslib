# Successor Power of Two

**Claim.** For every natural number $a$, $2^{a+1}=2\cdot 2^a$.

**Proof.** By the successor law for exponentiation, $2^{a+1}=2^a\cdot2$. Commutativity of multiplication gives $2\cdot2^a$.
