# Vanishing at zero

**Claim.** The real-valued expression $x^2+x+\log(1-x)$ equals $0$ at $x=0$.

**Proof.** At $x=0$, the two polynomial terms vanish, and $\log(1)=0$. Thus $0^2+0+\log(1-0)=0$.
