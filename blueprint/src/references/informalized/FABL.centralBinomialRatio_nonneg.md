# Nonnegative central binomial ratio

**Claim.** For every natural number $j$, $0\le \binom{2j}{j}/2^{2j}$.

**Proof.** A binomial coefficient is a nonnegative natural number, hence its real cast is nonnegative. The denominator $2^{2j}$ is positive, so their quotient is nonnegative.
