# Constant Pair Subspace Characterization

**Claim.** A vector x belongs to the constant pair subspace exactly when x is the zero vector or the all-ones vector.

**Proof.** The constant pair subspace has carrier {0,1}. Membership in that carrier is exactly the disjunction x=0 or x=1, as claimed.
