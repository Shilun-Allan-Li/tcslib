# Stability Range

**Claim.** For all real $\rho,\alpha,\beta$, Gaussian bivariate stability lies in the interval $[0,1]$.

**Proof.** The bivariate stability is the real measure of a measurable event under the product of two standard Gaussian probability measures. A measure of an event is nonnegative, and under a probability measure it is at most one. These two measure bounds give the claimed interval.
