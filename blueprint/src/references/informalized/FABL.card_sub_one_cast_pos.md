# Positivity after subtracting one

**Claim.** If a finite set $\Omega$ has at least two elements, then the real number obtained by subtracting $1$ from $|\Omega|$ is strictly positive.

**Proof.** Since $2\le |\Omega|$, we have $1<|\Omega|$. Subtracting one from this natural-number cardinality leaves a positive natural number. Its image in $\mathbb R$ is therefore strictly positive as well.
