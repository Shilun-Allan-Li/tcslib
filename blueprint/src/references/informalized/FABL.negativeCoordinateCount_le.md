# Negative Coordinate Bound

**Claim.** For every point $x$ of the $n$-dimensional sign cube, the number of coordinates equal to $-1$ is at most $n$.

**Proof.** The coordinates equal to $-1$ form a subset of all coordinates. Hence their number is at most the cardinality of the full coordinate set, which is $n$.
