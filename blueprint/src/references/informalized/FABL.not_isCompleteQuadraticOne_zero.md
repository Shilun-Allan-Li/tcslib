# Zero-Dimensional Quadratic Bit

**Claim.** For the unique Boolean input of length zero, the complete quadratic bit is not equal to $1$ in the field with two elements.

**Proof.** In dimension zero there are no indices to contribute to the defining sum for the complete quadratic bit. That empty sum is zero in the field with two elements, so the bit is not equal to one.
