# Singleton Mass Under the Uniform Bit Law

**Claim.** Each Boolean outcome has mass $1/2$ under the uniform probability law on the Booleans.

**Proof.** The uniform law assigns a subset real measure equal to its cardinality divided by the cardinality of the whole space. A singleton has cardinality one, and the Boolean space has cardinality two. Therefore the singleton mass is $1/2$.
