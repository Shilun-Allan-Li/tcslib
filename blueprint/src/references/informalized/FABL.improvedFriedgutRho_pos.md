# Positivity of Rho

**Claim.** For every real number $\eta>0$, the quantity $\rho(\eta)=\eta/(2+\eta)$ is positive.

**Proof.** Both the numerator $\eta$ and denominator $2+\eta$ are positive, so their quotient is positive.
