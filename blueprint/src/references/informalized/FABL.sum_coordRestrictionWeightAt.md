# Total coordinate restriction weight

**Claim.** For a real number $\delta$, the weights $\delta$, $(1-\delta)/2$, and $(1-\delta)/2$ of the free, fixed-to-$1$, and fixed-to-$-1$ restrictions sum to $1$.

**Proof.** The three restrictions exhaust the possibilities, so the sum of their weights is $\delta+(1-\delta)/2+(1-\delta)/2$. The two fixed-restriction terms combine to $1-\delta$, and the total is $1$.
