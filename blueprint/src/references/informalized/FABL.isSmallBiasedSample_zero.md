# Small bias in dimension zero

**Claim.** Every sample in the zero-dimensional binary cube is $\varepsilon$-biased, for any real $\varepsilon$.

**Proof.** Take any frequency $\gamma$. Since the zero-dimensional cube has only one element, namely $0$, we have $\gamma=0$. Thus the required hypothesis that $\gamma$ be nonzero is impossible, and the defining condition holds vacuously.
