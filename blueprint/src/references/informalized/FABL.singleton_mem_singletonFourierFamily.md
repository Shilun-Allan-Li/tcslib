# Singletons in the Singleton Fourier Family

**Claim.** For every coordinate $i\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, the singleton set $\{i\}$ belongs to the family of all singleton subsets of that coordinate set.

**Proof.** The family is the image of the full coordinate set under the map sending each coordinate $j$ to $\{j\}$. Taking $j=i$ shows that $\{i\}$ belongs to this image.
