# Positive Reciprocal Perpendicular Size

**Claim.** For a linear subspace $A$ of $\mathbb F_2^n$, the reciprocal of $2$ raised to the dimension of its orthogonal complement is positive.

**Proof.** The dimension of the orthogonal complement is a natural number, so the corresponding power of $2$ is positive. Its reciprocal is therefore positive as well.
