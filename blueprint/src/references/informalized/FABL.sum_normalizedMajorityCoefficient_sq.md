# Sum of squared normalized majority coefficients

**Claim.** For every natural number $m$, the sum over $2m+1$ coordinates of the squares of $1/\sqrt{2m+1}$ equals $1$.

**Proof.** There are $2m+1$ identical summands. Since $2m+1>0$, the square root is nonzero, and each squared reciprocal square root equals $1/(2m+1)$. Thus the sum is $(2m+1)/(2m+1)=1$.
