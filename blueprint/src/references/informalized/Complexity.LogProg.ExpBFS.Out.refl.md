# Reflexivity Outside a Finite Interval

**Claim.** A Boolean-valued function $M$ agrees with itself at every integer outside the set of integers $b+v$ with $v<K$.

**Proof.** For any integer outside that set, the two values being compared are both $M$ at that integer. They are therefore equal.
